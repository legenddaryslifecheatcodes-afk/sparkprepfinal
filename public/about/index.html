About SparkPrep

SparkPrep is an all-in-one self-publishing suite. You design your cover, format your pages, check both against your printer's rules, fix the problems and download print-ready files, all in one place. Power without complexity.

SparkPrep in one sentence: SparkPrep, made by Legenddary in Sacramento, California, helps self-published authors get their book files accepted the first time by Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu, and explains every problem in plain English.

Why it exists

Founder Joshua Davis wrote a book, Legenddary Mindset: The Awakening. It failed to print 18 times. Each rejection email used words he had never heard: bleed, safety area, ink coverage, PDF/X-1a. He built SparkPrep to fix his own files, then realized almost every first-time author runs into the same wall.

What SparkPrep does

  • Cover Studio (free): a cover maker with 224 templates and 333 fonts, sized to your printer's real cover rules, spine included.
  • Interior Studio (free): a page formatter with Word import, 11 book styles and a live preview of your printed pages.
  • AI writing help: spelling, grammar and wording suggestions.
  • Print check (preflight): checks your whole cover and every page for size, bleed, margins, spine width, colors, the 240% ink limit, picture sharpness and fonts.
  • Fixes: fixes what it safely can for you, and tells you plainly when something needs new art.
  • Print-ready export: PDF/X-1a files and a SparkPrep Certified report when every check passes.
