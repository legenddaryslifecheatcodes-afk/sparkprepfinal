Book formatting for KDP and IngramSpark, in plain English

Formatting is what turns your manuscript into a file a printer will accept. Here is what it covers, what it costs, and how to know if yours is right.

What is book formatting? Book formatting means setting up your book file so a printer can print it: the right trim size, margins and gutter, embedded fonts, 300 DPI pictures, page numbers and chapter starts, plus a cover with the right spine width and bleed. If any of it is off, Amazon KDP or IngramSpark sends your book back.

Is something wrong with your book's formatting?

If you've seen any of these, it's a formatting problem, and it can be fixed:

  • KDP or IngramSpark rejected your book or sent it back
  • KDP says your cover is the wrong size (fix)
  • Your text is too close to the edge (fix)
  • A message says a font isn't embedded (fix)
  • Your pictures look blurry (fix)
  • Your page count in Word doesn't match your PDF (why)

What book formatting covers

Inside your book (interior)

  • Page size that matches your trim size, like 6 x 9 inches
  • Margins, including the inside margin near the spine (the gutter), which grows with page count on KDP
  • Fonts embedded in the PDF
  • Pictures at 300 DPI, grayscale in a black and white book
  • Page numbers, chapter starts and front matter

Your cover (exterior)

  • The full wrap: back cover, spine and front cover as one file
  • Spine width that matches your page count and paper
  • Bleed: 0.125 inch of extra art past each trimmed edge
  • Text inside the safe zone so nothing gets cut off
  • A clear area for the barcode and ISBN

Your options, and what they really cost

  • Do it yourself in Word, Google Docs or Kindle Create: free, but it's easy to miss the problems that get books rejected.
  • Hire a book formatter: Reedsy reports an average of about $800 for interior formatting, plus about $930 (median) for a cover. Expect to wait on someone else's schedule.
  • Formatting software like Vellum or Atticus: a one-time purchase, but you still have to learn it, and it doesn't check your cover against the printer's rules.

Common questions

How much does book formatting cost?

Reedsy reports an average of about $800 for professional interior formatting. Doing it yourself is free. Legenddary's SparkPrep's audit is free through December 31, 2026, and its fix-my-file service is $29 for one file or $49 for both.

Can I format my book for free?

Yes, with Word, Google Docs, Amazon's free Kindle Create or SparkPrep's free Interior Studio. The hard part is knowing if it will pass the printer's checks, which is what a free audit tells you.

Do I need a book formatter?

Not always. A mostly-text book can often be formatted yourself and then checked. If you want it handled for you, a formatter or SparkPrep's fix service can do it.

Does book formatting work the same for KDP and IngramSpark?

Mostly, but not exactly. IngramSpark asks for a 0.5 inch safety margin, a 240% ink limit and PDF/X-1a files, and its spine width differs from KDP's. Check each file against each printer's own rules.
