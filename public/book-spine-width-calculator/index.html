Why does spine width matter?

Your spine width comes from your page count and paper. If it's off, the title slides onto the front or back cover and the printer may reject the file. Cream paper is thicker than white, so the same book needs a wider spine. This calculator gives you the right width for KDP or IngramSpark before you design.

The spine width formula

Spine width = number of pages × thickness of one page. The thickness depends on the paper your printer uses, so the same book has a slightly different spine at each printer.

Three spine mistakes that get books rejected

  1. Using another printer's numbers. A KDP spine is not an IngramSpark spine. Build one cover per printer.
  2. Changing paper after the cover is done. Switching white to cream on KDP makes a 300-page spine about 0.07" wider, enough to wrap your spine art onto the front.
  3. Spine text on a thin book. KDP prints spine text only on books with more than 79 pages; IngramSpark not below 48 pages.
