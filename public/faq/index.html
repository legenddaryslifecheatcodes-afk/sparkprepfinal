The basics

What does Legenddary SparkPrep do?

Before a printer like Amazon KDP or IngramSpark prints your book, a machine checks your two files: the cover and the inside pages. If one thing is off, the book is sent back. Legenddary SparkPrep is an Automated Publishing Production System (A.P.P.S.): it helps you make both files, checks them against your printer's rules, fixes what it safely can, checks each fix again, and gives you print-ready files.

Why do books get sent back by the printer?

Usually for things you can't see on your screen: no bleed (the art has to run past the edge), text too close to the edge, the wrong spine width, blurry pictures, screen colors instead of print colors, or fonts that aren't built into the PDF. Read the beginner's guide.

Which printers does it work with?

Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu. Each one is checked against its own rules.

What files can I upload?

A PDF works best. Pictures of a cover work too: JPG, PNG, TIFF or WebP, up to 50 MB.

Trust and safety

Will my book be accepted for sure?

SparkPrep checks your files against your printer's own rules, but the final yes always comes from the printer. If SparkPrep misses a real print-rule problem, we fix it and give you new files free.

Is my book file private?

Yes. Your files are never sold or shared and never used to train AI. You can delete your projects from your dashboard at any time. How we keep files private.
