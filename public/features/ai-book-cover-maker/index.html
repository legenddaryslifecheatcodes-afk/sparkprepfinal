AI Book Cover Maker

Start with an idea and let AI help design your cover, or pick one of 224 free templates. Either way, Legenddary SparkPrep builds the whole cover, front, spine and back, to your printer's exact size rules from the very first click.

01

Meet the feature

A print cover is not just a front picture. It is one wide file: back cover, spine and front cover side by side, plus a strip of extra color around the edge (the "bleed") that gets cut off. The spine's width depends on your page count and paper. Get any of that wrong and Amazon KDP or IngramSpark sends the file back.

The AI Book Cover Maker lives inside the Legenddary SparkPrep Cover Studio. It solves two problems at once: it helps you make a cover that looks good, and it makes sure that cover is the right size and shape to print.

  • Start from an idea: tell the AI designer what your book is about, and it plans the layout, colors and fonts and can paint artwork for the whole wrap.
  • Or start from a template: 224 free templates (14 layouts in 16 styles), sorted into 12 genres, with 333 free fonts.
  • Printer rules built in: guide lines show the trim, the bleed, the spine folds, the safe area and the barcode spot, and a live print check flags words that are in the wrong place.

How it works

  1. Size first. You choose (or keep the default) printer, binding, trim size, paper and page count. Legenddary SparkPrep reads that printer's own size rules and builds a canvas the exact size of the full cover, with bleed. No guessing at the spine.
  2. Design on top of the real shape. Templates are built in inches against the real back, spine and front panels, so they fit any size. The AI designer picks from the same layouts and styles, so its covers fit too.
  3. Words stay words. The AI only paints artwork. Your title, author name and back-cover text are always real, editable text, because AI picture tools often misspell words.
  4. Guides and a live check. Red shows the bleed that gets cut off, gold the trim, blue the spine folds, green the safe area, and a box marks the barcode spot. Guides never print. The print check flags words outside the safe area, words under the barcode, and pictures under about 200 DPI.
  5. Change your mind any time. If your page count changes, the spine changes and your design moves with it. A cover that was sent to a book can be re-fitted to the new spine.
  6. Print-ready when you are. Designing is free. "Send to my book" passes the cover at 300 DPI into the Legenddary SparkPrep A.P.P.S., where it is checked against the printer's rules, fixed where it safely can be, checked again, and exported as a print-ready file. That file is part of buying the book.

Common questions

Is the AI book cover maker free?

Designing is free: the Cover Studio, all 224 templates, 333 fonts and the print check cost nothing, with no sign-up. The AI designer and the full-size print-ready cover file are included when you buy a book.

Will the AI misspell my book title?

No. The AI only paints the artwork. Your title, author name and back-cover words are set as real, editable text by the Cover Studio, because AI picture tools often misspell words.

Which printers does the cover maker size covers for?

Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu. Pick your printer, binding, trim size, paper and page count, and the front, spine and back are sized to that printer's own numbers. If you change the page count later, the spine and your design move with it.
