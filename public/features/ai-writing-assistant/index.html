AI Writing Assistant

A proofreader built into your book editor. It finds spelling, grammar and punctuation mistakes, and points out wording that could read smoother. It only suggests. You decide, one change at a time.

01

Meet the feature

After months of writing, you stop seeing your own mistakes. Your eyes read what you meant to write, not what's on the page. Readers notice. Hiring a human proofreader is worth it for many authors, but it takes time and money, and you still want your draft as clean as possible first.

The AI Writing Assistant lives in the Writing help tab of the Legenddary SparkPrep Interior Studio, right next to your words. It has two buttons:

  • Fix spelling & grammar: catches typos, wrong words that sound alike (there and their), grammar slips like "would of", and missing punctuation.
  • Improve my wording: points out word choice, clearer phrasing, repeated words and sentences that could flow better. These are ideas, not orders.
  • Plus a free spell check: red underlines under misspelled words as you type, free for everyone.

What makes it different from pasting your book into a chatbot: it never rewrites your book behind your back. Every suggestion is one exact change to one exact spot, underlined in your text, with Accept and Skip buttons. Your voice stays yours.

03

How it works

  1. You choose what to check. The chapter your cursor is in, just the words you've selected, or the whole book. A whole book is sent in parts of about 8,000 characters, so long books work, and you can press Stop at any time.
  2. The AI reads like a proofreader. Your paragraphs go to the AI, which sends back exact changes: these words, at this spot, become these words.
  3. Every suggestion is double-checked. Before it's shown, Legenddary SparkPrep makes sure the words it wants to change are really there, exactly as written. If you edited that sentence while the check was running, the suggestion is dropped instead of landing in the wrong place.
  4. Underlines, not edits. Suggestions appear as underlines on top of your text. They are not part of your book, so if you ignore them, nothing in your book changes.
  5. You accept, one undo step each. Accept changes exactly the underlined words. Ctrl+Z undoes it. Accept all fixes makes every spelling, grammar and punctuation fix in one step (wording ideas are never accepted in bulk).
  6. Then on to print. Your corrected writing is laid out as real printed pages in the Interior Formatter, and "Send to my book" takes it into the Legenddary SparkPrep A.P.P.S. for the print check, fixes and print-ready PDF.

Honest limits: the AI is a strong second pair of eyes, not a replacement for a human editor's judgment about story, pacing or facts. It can miss things, and it can suggest something you disagree with. That's why nothing changes until you say so.

Common questions

Will the AI rewrite my book?

No. It only suggests small, exact changes, like one word or one phrase, and shows each one before anything happens. Nothing in your book changes until you press Accept, and Ctrl+Z undoes any change you accept.

Is the AI writing assistant free?

The spell check in the editor is free, with no sign-up. The AI suggestions (Fix spelling & grammar and Improve my wording) need a free account and come with a Legenddary SparkPrep book, with a daily allowance of 400,000 characters per account. See pricing.

Can it check my whole book at once?

Yes. Choose the chapter you're in, just the words you've selected, or your whole book. A whole book is checked in parts of about 8,000 characters, and you can stop at any time.
