Automated Book Preflight

Find out why your book would be rejected before you submit it. Upload your cover or PDF, and Legenddary SparkPrep checks it against your printer's own rules and shows every problem, where it is, and which rule it breaks.

What is a book preflight check?

A preflight check reads your print file the way the printer's computer will, before you submit it. It checks trim size, bleed, margins, fonts, picture resolution, color, ink and the PDF format, and lists anything that would get the book rejected or printed wrong.

01

Meet the feature

Amazon KDP and IngramSpark often reject a file with a short note like "resolution insufficient" and no details. Then you guess, fix, upload again and wait again. Our founder's own book failed to print 18 times. That's why this check exists.

The Automated Book Preflight checks one printer's rules at a time, using that printer's own numbers: Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu. It checks:

  • Size: trim size, bleed, and the full cover size (back, spine and front) with the right spine width for your page count and paper.
  • Safe areas: margins, the gutter by the spine, the safe zone, and the barcode and ISBN area.
  • Print quality: picture resolution (300 DPI), CMYK color and the 240% ink limit.
  • The file itself: embedded fonts and the PDF/X-1a format printers expect.

Each problem comes with three things: what failed and why it gets rejected, the exact printer rule it breaks, and where it is in your file.

How it works

  1. One printer's rulebook. You pick the printer and size. The check uses only that printer's own numbers from its own spec sheets and templates, never another printer's.
  2. It reads the real file. Page sizes, the fonts inside the PDF, every picture's real resolution, the color mode, the ink coverage and the PDF format flags. Covers are measured as one full wrap, with the spine width worked out from your page count and paper.
  3. It points, it doesn't guess. Each problem comes with the rule it breaks and where it is: a page, a region, a picture, or a measurement like "8.5 x 11 inches, expected 6 x 9".
  4. It only detects. The check never changes your file. That's on purpose: you see the truth about your file first.
  5. Then the fixes. When you make your book with Legenddary SparkPrep, the Automated Publishing Production System (A.P.P.S.) fixes what it safely can (size, bleed, color, ink, fonts, PDF/X-1a), checks every fix again, and only asks you for new art when a fix would damage yours.

Free check limits: the free check covers the whole cover, or page 1 of your inside pages. The Advanced Audit checks every inside page (up to 300), and so does a Legenddary SparkPrep book. Passing our check is a strong sign, but the printer always makes the final call.

Common questions

What is a preflight check for a book?

A preflight check reads your print file the way a printer's computer does, before you submit it. It checks trim size, bleed, margins, fonts, picture resolution, color and ink, and the PDF format, and lists anything that would get the file rejected or printed wrong. Want the long version? Read what preflight is, for first-time authors.

Does the preflight check fix my file?

No. The check only finds problems and shows where they are. Fixing happens when you make your book with Legenddary SparkPrep: it fixes what it safely can, checks every fix again, and gives you print-ready files. See pricing.

How much of my book does the free check cover?

The free check covers the whole cover (back, spine and front) or page 1 of your inside pages, and needs only your email. The Advanced Audit checks every inside page, up to 300, and so does a Legenddary SparkPrep book.

Is my book file private?

Yes. Your file is only used to run your check. It is never sold, shared or used to train AI. Read the privacy page.
