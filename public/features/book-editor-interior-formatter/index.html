Book Editor & Interior Formatter

Write your book, or bring it in from Word, and watch it turn into real printed pages as you go. Pick a book style, and Legenddary SparkPrep sets the fonts, chapter openings and margins to your printer's rules for you.

01

Meet the feature

A Word file is not a printed book. Printers want every page the exact trim size, margins that are wide enough for your page count (with extra room by the spine), page numbers, a title page, and chapters that start on a fresh page. Doing that by hand in Word is where most first-time authors get stuck, or get rejected.

The Book Editor & Interior Formatter is the Legenddary SparkPrep Interior Studio. On the left you write like you would in Word. On the right you see the actual printed pages, laid out the way the printer will print them.

  • Write or import: type straight in, or use Import Word (.docx) to bring in your chapters, headings, quotes, scene breaks and pictures.
  • 11 book styles: Classic Novel, Modern Fiction, Literary, Romance, Thriller & Mystery, Fantasy & Epic, Non-fiction, Memoir, Workbook, Large Print and Poetry. Switch any time; your words stay the same.
  • Printer rules built in: Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu, in the common trim sizes. Margins are never below that printer's own minimum for your page count.
  • The book parts done for you: title page, copyright page, dedication, a table of contents with the real page numbers, page numbers and the names at the top of each page.

03

How it works

  1. Size and printer first. You pick the printer and trim size. That sets the page size and which margin table to follow.
  2. Write in a simple editor. Mark chapter titles and section titles, add quotes, lists, scene breaks, page breaks and pictures. Or import a Word file: Word's page size, margins and fonts are left behind on purpose, so the book style and printer rules can take over.
  3. Real typesetting, not a screenshot of Word. Legenddary SparkPrep's server lays out every page with the style's embedded, free-to-use fonts. Chapters start on a new page, and the contents list gets the real page numbers.
  4. Margins you never have to look up. Margins are never below your printer's own minimum for your page count, or a 0.5" safe margin. The exact numbers are shown above the preview, so nothing is hidden.
  5. Honest warnings. If your book is shorter than the printer's minimum page count, the studio tells you.
  6. On to print. Writing, styling and previewing are free. "Send to my book" typesets your pages into your book inside the Legenddary SparkPrep A.P.P.S., where they are checked against the printer's rules, fixed where it safely can, checked again, and exported as a print-ready PDF. That file is part of buying the book.

Common questions

Can I import my Word document?

Yes. Import Word (.docx) brings in your chapters, headings, bold and italic, lists, quotes, page breaks, scene breaks and pictures. Word's own page size, margins and fonts are left behind on purpose, because the book style and your printer's rules set those.

What margins does the interior formatter use?

Never smaller than your printer's own minimum for your page count, and never smaller than a 0.5 inch safe margin. The inside margin by the spine grows automatically as your book gets longer. The exact numbers are shown above the page preview. Want to check numbers yourself? Try the book margin calculator.

Is the book editor free?

Yes. Writing, importing, choosing a style and previewing your printed pages are free, with no sign-up; your work is saved in your browser. The print-ready PDF is part of buying the book, and a free account lets you save it to your books.
