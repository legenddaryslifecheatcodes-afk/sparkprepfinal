Cover Template Designer

224 free cover templates that are built for your book, not a stock size. Type your title once and see it on every template. Pick one, and it becomes a full printed cover: back, spine and front, at your exact size.

What makes a cover template "print-ready"?

A print cover is one wide image: back, spine and front, plus bleed. Its size depends on your trim size, and the spine depends on your page count and paper. A template is only print-ready if it is built to those exact numbers and keeps every word inside the printer's safe area. These are.

Meet the feature

Most free cover templates are a pretty front cover at one fixed size. Then you find out your printer wants a full wrap with a spine that's 0.451" wide (or 0.6", or 1.1"), and the template doesn't fit. You start over, or pay someone.

The Cover Template Designer works the other way around. It starts from your book's real numbers, then draws the template around them:

  • 224 templates: 14 layouts (Classic, Big Type, Band, Emblem, Picture Window and more) in 16 color-and-font styles, sorted into 12 genres.
  • Your words on every thumbnail: type your title, subtitle and author once, and every template preview shows them, so you choose by how your book looks.
  • Swap without retyping: try a different template and your words carry over.
  • Re-fits when your book changes: new trim size or page count? The spine resizes and every word moves to the same spot on its panel, still inside the safe area.

How it works

  1. Your book's numbers come first. Printer, binding, trim size, paper and page count give the full cover size and spine width. Legenddary SparkPrep's spec service works them out from each printer's own numbers.
  2. Templates are recipes, not pictures. Each of the 224 is a set of instructions in inches: "title 11% of the front's width, centered, 64% down." So it can be drawn at any size, and the words stay real, editable text.
  3. Built to the printer's lines. Words are placed inside the safe area, the background runs into the bleed, and the barcode area on the back is left empty.
  4. Make it yours. Change any word, color or font (333 free, open-licensed fonts), add shapes or your own picture. The print check watches as you go.
  5. Re-fit, don't restart. When the size changes, every object moves to the same spot on its own panel, words are kept inside the new safe area, and the spine text resizes to fit.
  6. On to print. "Send to my book" puts the cover into your book in the Legenddary SparkPrep A.P.P.S., where it is checked, fixed if needed and exported as a print-ready file.

Common questions

What is a full-wrap book cover template?

A full-wrap cover is one image that wraps around a printed book: back cover, spine and front cover, plus bleed around the edge. Its width depends on your trim size and your spine, and the spine depends on your page count and paper. Legenddary SparkPrep builds every template to those exact numbers. Check your own numbers with the spine width calculator.

Are the cover templates really free?

Yes. All 224 templates, the 333 fonts and the design tools are free to use, always. You can download a free preview image. The full-size print-ready cover file comes with a Legenddary SparkPrep book (see pricing).

What happens to my template if my page count or trim size changes?

The cover re-fits. Every word and shape moves to the same spot on its own panel, the spine gets wider or narrower, and words stay inside the safe area. You don't start over.
