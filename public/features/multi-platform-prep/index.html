Multi-Platform Prep

Selling on Amazon and in bookstores? Then the same book goes to more than one printer, and each one wants a different file. Pick the printer and Legenddary SparkPrep sizes your book to that printer's own numbers.

Can one cover file work for KDP and IngramSpark?

Usually not. The design can be the same, but the file size can't. Each printer uses a different paper thickness, so the spine is a different width. A 6" x 9", 200-page book on white paper needs a 0.451" spine at KDP and 0.391" at IngramSpark. Each printer needs its own cover file.

01

Meet the feature

Many authors publish on Amazon KDP to sell on Amazon, and on IngramSpark to reach bookstores and libraries. Some add Lulu or Barnes & Noble Press. It's the same book, but not the same rules.

The usual mistake: you make one cover, it gets accepted at one printer, and the other sends it back because the spine is the wrong width or the barcode box is in the wrong place.

Multi-Platform Prep fixes that at the start:

  • Four printers built in: Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu.
  • Each printer's own numbers: spine width, cover size, barcode area and page rules come from that printer, never borrowed from another one.
  • Switch printers, keep your design: change the printer and the cover re-fits. Your words and layout stay.
  • Checked against the right rules: the book check and the fixes use the printer you picked.

03

How it works

  1. You pick the printer. In the Cover Studio, the Editor or the free book check, choose Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
  2. Its own numbers load. Paper thickness, bleed, safe margins, barcode area, page limits and the PDF type it wants. Legenddary SparkPrep never fills a gap with another printer's number.
  3. The cover is sized for it. The spine and full cover size are worked out for your page count and paper at that printer, and the design re-fits.
  4. The inside pages follow its rules. Safe margins are 0.375" for KDP and 0.5" for IngramSpark. IngramSpark covers are built for an even page count.
  5. Checked and fixed for that printer. The book check, Auto-Fix and the final file all use the printer you chose.
  6. Publishing to two printers? Set up a book for each one. In the Cover Studio, switching the printer re-fits the same design, so you only design once.

Common questions

Can I use the same book cover for KDP and IngramSpark?

Usually not. The front and back can look the same, but the file size is different because each printer's paper is a different thickness. A 6" x 9", 200-page book on white paper has a 0.451" spine at KDP and a 0.391" spine at IngramSpark, so each printer needs its own cover file.

Why is the spine width different at each printer?

Spine width comes from page count times how thick each sheet of paper is, and every printer uses its own paper. KDP uses 0.002252" per page for white paper, IngramSpark's white 50 lb paper is 512 pages per inch, and Lulu adds a flat 0.06" to its paperback formula.

Is the inside of the book different for each printer too?

Some rules are. Legenddary SparkPrep keeps words at least 0.375" from the edge for KDP and 0.5" for IngramSpark, and IngramSpark builds covers for an even page count, so a 197-page book is treated as 198. See the margin calculator for KDP's full margin table.
