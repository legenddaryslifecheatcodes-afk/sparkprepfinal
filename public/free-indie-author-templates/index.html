Quick answers

Where can I get free book templates for KDP?

SparkPrep has free cover templates sized for KDP, a printable KDP cover template generator, and a free interior layout tool. All are free to use.

Are these templates really free?

Yes. Designing with the templates, fonts and tools is free. A free preview download is included; the print-ready files come with a SparkPrep book.

Do the templates work for IngramSpark too?

Yes. Cover Studio and Interior Studio work for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu, using each printer's own numbers.
