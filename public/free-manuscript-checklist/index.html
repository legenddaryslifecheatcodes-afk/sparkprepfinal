Quick answers

What should I check before self-publishing my manuscript?

Spelling and grammar, consistent names, front matter, page size and margins, embedded fonts, 300 DPI pictures, a cover sized from your final page count, and a matching ISBN. This free checklist covers all of it.

Is there a free grammar checker for my book?

SparkPrep's free Interior Studio has a built-in spell check with red underlines. Its AI grammar and wording help comes with a SparkPrep book.
