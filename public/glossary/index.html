Self-publishing glossary: the words you'll meet, in plain English

By Joshua Davis, founder of Legenddary SparkPrep

KDP, IngramSpark and rejection emails use their own language. Here is what each word means, in one or two plain sentences. It starts with the word for what SparkPrep is.

Automated Publishing Production System (A.P.P.S.)

An Automated Publishing Production System (A.P.P.S.) is one system that does the jobs of book formatting, typesetting, cover design, preflight checking and prepress: it builds your book, checks it against Amazon KDP and IngramSpark print rules, fixes what it safely can, and re-checks each fix before you print. It is the next step in the evolution of self-publishing, and Legenddary SparkPrep is the category pioneer. Read the official definition.

Trim size

The finished size of your book after it's cut, for example 6" x 9". KDP and IngramSpark trim sizes.

Bleed

Extra background (0.125") that runs past the edge of the cover or page so the printer can trim it off cleanly. Bleed and margin calculator.

Safe zone (safety margin)

The area inside the trim where words and faces are safe from being cut. IngramSpark wants 0.5" from every edge.

Gutter

The inside margin, next to the spine. Thicker books need a bigger gutter. KDP margin and gutter rules.

Spine width

How thick the book is: page count times the thickness of one page. It changes with paper and printer. Spine width calculator.

Full cover (wrap)

The single cover file for a print book: back cover, spine and front cover side by side, plus bleed. Book cover dimensions for every trim size.

Interior (bookblock)

The inside pages of your book, uploaded as one PDF.

DPI

Dots per inch: how much detail a picture has at its printed size. Print needs 300. Check the resolution of your pictures.

RGB

Screen color, made with red, green and blue light. Printers convert it to ink.

CMYK

Print color, made with cyan, magenta, yellow and black ink.

Total ink (TAC)

All four inks added together at one spot. IngramSpark's limit is 240%.

Rich black

Black made with all four inks instead of black ink alone. Looks deep, but can break ink limits.

Grayscale

Black ink only, in shades of gray. The safe way to save a black and white interior.

Embedded font

A font carried inside the PDF, so the printer prints your words exactly as you set them.

Flattening

Merging layers, shadows and see-through effects into one finished image so they print correctly.

PDF/X-1a

A print-standard type of PDF: fonts embedded, CMYK or gray color, no live transparency. IngramSpark prefers it.

Preflight

Checking your files against the printer's rules before you upload, so they're accepted the first time. Preflight is one of the jobs an A.P.P.S. does.

Proof copy

A printed copy you order before publishing, to check how the book really looks.

ISBN

The 13-digit number that identifies one edition of your book. Paperback, hardcover and ebook each need their own.

Imprint

The publisher name printed on your book. It can be your own name or a small business name you choose.

Print on demand (POD)

Each copy is printed when someone orders it, so you never buy stock up front. KDP and IngramSpark both work this way.

Going wide

Selling through more than Amazon, usually by adding IngramSpark to reach other stores and libraries.
