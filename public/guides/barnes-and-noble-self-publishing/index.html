Barnes and Noble self-publishing: how B&N Press works in 2026

Updated November 16, 2026 · Barnes & Noble Press, for first-time self-publishers

Barnes & Noble Press lets you sell your book on BN.com and NOOK for free. The rules changed in 2026. Here's what it pays, what it doesn't do, and how to avoid a common scam.

How does Barnes and Noble self-publishing work? Barnes & Noble Press is free. You upload your book and sell it on BN.com and NOOK, with no exclusivity. Ebooks priced $0.99 to $9.99 earn 70%. Print books earn 55% minus printing, and since April 2026 must be priced at $14.99 or more. It does not put your book in stores automatically.

What is Barnes & Noble Press?

B&N Press is Barnes & Noble's free self-publishing service. You upload your book and sell it as an ebook on NOOK and as a print book on BN.com. You don't have to be exclusive, so you can also sell on Amazon and elsewhere (B&N Press).

What B&N Press pays

  • Ebooks: 70% royalty on ebooks priced $0.99 to $9.99.
  • Print books: 55% of the price, minus the cost of printing. B&N's own example: a 300-page 6" x 9" paperback at $14.99 earns about $3.40 per copy.
  • Payments: monthly once you've earned $10 or more.

The 2026 rule changes

  • $14.99 minimum price for print books since April 22, 2026. Cheaper print listings are removed.
  • 100 titles per account.
  • No public domain books. Only your own original work.

Source: B&N Press, May 2026 policy update.

Will my book be in Barnes & Noble stores?

Not automatically. B&N Press sells your book online. Store shelves are a separate decision made by B&N's buyers.

Watch out: Barnes & Noble says it will never ask authors for money in exchange for store placement. If someone "from Barnes & Noble" offers that for a fee, it's a scam.

How to publish on B&N Press, step by step

  1. Make a free account at press.barnesandnoble.com.
  2. Add your title, description and price (print: $14.99 or more).
  3. Upload your inside pages and your cover. Print books need a full cover (back, spine and front) sized for your page count.
  4. Order a proof copy if you can, and check it.
  5. Publish.

B&N's free ISBN can only be used at B&N. If you'll also sell through Amazon or IngramSpark, many authors buy their own ISBN.

Where most new authors get stuck

The upload. Print files must match B&N's size, margin and cover rules, and they differ from Amazon's. See how the printers' rules differ, or start with how to self-publish a book in 7 steps.
