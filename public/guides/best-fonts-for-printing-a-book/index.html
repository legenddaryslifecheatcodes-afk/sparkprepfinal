The best fonts for your printed book, and the size to use

Updated November 27, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark

Your font is the one thing a reader looks at on every page. You don't need a fancy one. You need one that's easy to read for hours, legal to use and properly saved inside your PDF.

What is the best font for a printed book? Use a simple serif font for the story, like Garamond, Palatino or Cambria, at about 10.5 to 12 point. Amazon KDP requires at least 7 point and suggests Garamond, Palatino Linotype, Centaur and its own Amazon Endure. Save fancy fonts for chapter titles, and embed every font in your PDF.

Fonts KDP suggests for body text

Amazon's KDP help says to pick something simple that's easy to read in long passages. It lists:

  • Garamond and Palatino Linotype: classic book fonts
  • Centaur and Hightower Text
  • Amazon Endure: KDP's own font, made to fit more words per page (and lower your printing cost). Download it from KDP's templates page.
  • Constantia and Cambria: also help keep the page count down

From KDP's Paperback Fonts page, checked November 2026. You're responsible for having the license for any font you use.

What size?

  • KDP's minimum is 7 point, on the inside pages and on the cover.
  • Most novels use about 10.5 to 12 point for the story. Smaller book sizes can go a little smaller; large print uses much bigger type, often 16 to 18 point.
  • Leave a little space between lines (about 1.15 to 1.3 line spacing) so the page doesn't feel crowded.

Font size changes your page count, and your page count changes your spine width and cover size. Pick your font before you design the cover. Estimate pages with the page count calculator, then see your cover size in the book cover dimensions chart.

The free-font trap

Fonts from free font websites cause a lot of rejections. KDP warns that some won't embed in a PDF for technical or legal reasons, some are missing letters or an italic version, and some are badly drawn. Fonts that are open-licensed (like the ones on Google Fonts) are a safer free choice.

Watch out for "fake bold" and "fake italic" too: if your font has no real bold version and Word makes one anyway, KDP can reject it as a faux font error.

Embed your fonts

"Embedding" means saving the font inside your PDF, so the printer has it. In Word: File, Options, Save, tick Embed fonts in the file, and make sure "Embed only the characters used" and "Do not embed common system fonts" are not ticked. To check, open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat, then File, Properties, Fonts: every font should say "Embedded" or "Embedded Subset". If your file came back with a font error, follow fix a font not embedded or fix a font licensing error.

Quick answers

What is the minimum font size for KDP?

7 point. Amazon KDP requires at least 7-point text on both the inside pages and the cover so it's readable.

Can I use free fonts in my book?

Yes, if the license allows it and the font embeds in your PDF. Many free-site fonts don't embed or are incomplete. Open-licensed fonts, like those on Google Fonts, are a safer choice.

Should I use a serif or sans-serif font for a novel?

Most printed novels use a serif font (with small strokes on the letters), like Garamond or Palatino, because it's easy on the eyes for long reading. Sans-serif fonts work well for headings and some nonfiction.
