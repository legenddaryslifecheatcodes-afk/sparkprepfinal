Children's picture books: the right size, pictures and cover for your first one

Updated November 22, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark

Picture books are full of color art that runs right to the edge of the page, which makes them the easiest kind of book to get rejected. Get three things right — size, picture quality and bleed — and most problems disappear.

What size is a self-published children's picture book? Popular picture-book sizes on Amazon KDP are 8.5 x 8.5, 8.25 x 8.25, 8 x 10 and 8.5 x 11 inches. Each illustration needs about 300 DPI: a full 8.5 x 8.5 inch page with bleed needs art about 2,588 x 2,625 pixels. Pages with art to the edge need 0.125 inch bleed.

Step 2: Pick the color paper

KDP offers standard color (cheaper, needs at least 72 pages) and premium color (richer, from 24 pages). Most picture books are 24 to 40 pages, so on KDP they usually need premium color.

Page ranges from KDP's trim size table, checked November 2026.

Step 3: Make sure the art is big enough

Ask your illustrator for files at 300 DPI at the full printed size, including bleed. Multiply inches by 300:

  • Full page, 8.5 x 8.5 book with bleed (8.625 x 8.75 inches): about 2,588 x 2,625 pixels
  • Two-page spread, same book: about 5,175 x 2,625 pixels
  • Full page, 8 x 10 with bleed (8.125 x 10.25 inches): about 2,438 x 3,075 pixels

Got art already? Check it in seconds with the free image DPI checker. New to this? Read what DPI means.

Step 4: Set up bleed and the safe zone

Art that touches the edge must run 0.125 inch past it. Words, and faces your reader must see, should stay at least 0.375 inch inside the edge, and further from the middle fold. More: what bleed is.

Step 5: The cover

Start with a free children's book cover template made for your size, then find the exact cover size (front, spine and back) in the book cover dimensions chart. Thin picture books have narrow spines, often too narrow for any spine text.

Quick answers

How many pages is a children's picture book?

Most picture books are 24 to 40 pages, and 32 is the traditional length. KDP premium color paperbacks start at 24 pages; standard color needs at least 72.

What DPI should children's book illustrations be?

300 DPI at the size they'll print, including bleed. A full 8.5 x 8.5 inch page with bleed needs art about 2,588 x 2,625 pixels.

Do children's books need bleed?

Yes, almost always. Any page where the art touches the edge needs 0.125 inch of bleed, and if one page needs it the whole file must be set up with bleed.
