Do you need an ISBN to self-publish? Free vs paid, in plain English

Updated November 18, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Bowker (U.S.)

An ISBN is the 13-digit number that identifies your book. New authors often think they must buy one before anything else. You might not need to. Here is the simple version.

Do I need an ISBN to self-publish a book? For a print book, yes, but it can be free. Amazon KDP and IngramSpark (U.S. authors) both give free ISBNs. A KDP ebook needs no ISBN at all. Buy your own from Bowker ($125 for one, $295 for ten) only if you want your own publisher name on the book and full control.

What an ISBN is

ISBN stands for International Standard Book Number. It is a 13-digit number that tells stores and libraries exactly which book, which edition and which format they are ordering. It is usually printed above the barcode on your back cover.

  • Each format needs its own ISBN: paperback, hardcover and ebook are three different numbers.
  • An ISBN does not give you copyright. You own your writing either way.
  • ISBNs never expire, so you can buy some now and use them later.

What about ebooks?

A Kindle ebook on KDP does not need an ISBN. Amazon gives it its own number, called an ASIN. Other ebook stores and IngramSpark ask for one, and it must be different from your print ISBN.

So which should a first-time author pick?

  • Just testing the waters on Amazon? The free KDP ISBN is fine.
  • Want bookstores and libraries too? Buying 10 ISBNs ($295) is the most common choice. It covers a paperback, hardcover and ebook, with numbers left over for your next book, and you can use the same number on KDP and IngramSpark.
  • Building a brand or a series? Own your ISBNs, so your publisher name is the same on every book.

Quick answers

How much does an ISBN cost?

In the U.S., Bowker charges $125 for one ISBN, $295 for ten and $575 for one hundred. KDP and IngramSpark (for U.S. authors) also offer free ISBNs, but they can only be used with that company.

Can I use the same ISBN for my paperback and ebook?

No. Every format needs its own ISBN. A paperback, a hardcover and an ebook are three different numbers.

Can I use a free KDP ISBN on IngramSpark?

No. A free KDP ISBN can only be used on Amazon. To sell the same paperback through IngramSpark, use your own ISBN on both.
