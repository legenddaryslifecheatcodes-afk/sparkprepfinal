Ebook cover size: the Kindle cover dimensions, in plain English

Updated November 18, 2026 · Amazon KDP (Kindle), other ebook stores

An ebook cover is much simpler than a print cover. It's just the front, as one picture. But it has its own size, and it is not the same as your paperback cover. Here are the numbers.

What size should an ebook cover be? For Kindle, Amazon recommends 1,600 pixels wide by 2,560 pixels tall, saved as a JPEG in RGB color. That is a 1 to 1.6 shape. The file must be under 50 MB and no more than 10,000 pixels on a side. It is the front only: no spine, back cover or barcode.

Can I use my paperback front cover as my ebook cover?

Yes, with one change. A 6 x 9 inch paperback front is 1 wide to 1.5 tall. Kindle's best shape is a little taller (1 to 1.6). So either add a bit of background at the top and bottom, or save it at 1,600 x 2,400 pixels. Amazon will still show it. Just cut off the bleed and spine first, so only the front is left.

Mistakes that get ebook covers rejected

  • Too small or blurry. Text that is fuzzy or cut off fails Amazon's check.
  • Words that don't match. The title and author name on the cover must match your book details exactly.
  • Prices or sale words like "only 99 cents" or "free today."
  • Leftover print parts: a spine, back cover or barcode.
  • A white cover with no edge. It disappears on Amazon's white page. Amazon suggests a thin gray border (3 to 4 pixels).

Quick answers

What is the best size for a Kindle cover?

1,600 pixels wide by 2,560 pixels tall. Amazon says covers at least 2,500 pixels tall look best on high-definition screens.

What DPI should an ebook cover be?

Amazon recommends at least 300 DPI. For an ebook, the pixel size (1,600 x 2,560) matters more than the DPI number.

Does an ebook cover need bleed?

No. Bleed is only for printed covers, because the printer trims the edges. An ebook cover is shown on a screen, so nothing is trimmed.

Can my ebook cover be a PNG?

Yes. KDP accepts JPEG, PNG, TIFF and non-moving GIF files. JPEG is Amazon's first choice. SVG files are not accepted.
