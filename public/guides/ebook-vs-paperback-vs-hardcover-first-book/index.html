Ebook, paperback or hardcover: which should your first book be?

Updated November 23, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark

You don't have to choose just one. But each format needs different files, and doing all three at once is where many first-time authors get stuck. Here's what each one takes, and an easy order to do them in.

Should I publish an ebook or paperback first? Most first-time authors do best publishing the paperback and ebook together, then a hardcover later. All three are free to publish on Amazon KDP. The ebook is easiest; the paperback needs a print-ready PDF and a full wrap cover; a hardcover needs 75 to 550 pages and its own cover file.

Ebook: the easiest start

An ebook has no page size, no margins and no spine. Your text re-flows to fit each reader's screen. You only need a clean manuscript and a front cover image.

Paperback: what most authors want to hold

A paperback needs a fixed-size PDF (your trim size), correct margins, pictures at 300 DPI, and a full wrap cover whose spine width depends on your page count and paper. This is where most rejections happen, so it's worth checking before you upload. Cover sizes for every format are in the book cover dimensions chart.

Hardcover: a nice extra, later

A hardcover needs a different cover file and a book of 75 to 550 pages on KDP. If you plan one, choose a paperback size that KDP also offers in hardcover (5.5 x 8.5, 6 x 9, 6.14 x 9.21, 7 x 10 or 8.25 x 11), so you can reuse your interior. Bookstores and libraries also like hardcovers through IngramSpark, which needs its own jacket rules.

An easy order for your first book

  1. Finish and edit your manuscript.
  2. Publish the paperback and ebook close together. Amazon shows both formats on one book page.
  3. Order a proof copy and read it on paper.
  4. Add a hardcover once the paperback is right.

Each format needs its own ISBN if you buy your own; see do I need an ISBN?

Quick answers

Is it free to publish a paperback on Amazon?

Yes. KDP charges nothing up front for a paperback or hardcover. The printing cost is taken out of each sale, and you keep the royalty.

Do I need a different cover for the ebook and paperback?

Yes. The ebook cover is the front only, about 2,560 x 1,600 pixels. The paperback cover is one file with the back, spine and front plus bleed, sized for your trim size and page count.

How many pages does a KDP hardcover need?

Between 75 and 550 pages, in one of five sizes: 5.5 x 8.5, 6 x 9, 6.14 x 9.21, 7 x 10 or 8.25 x 11 inches.
