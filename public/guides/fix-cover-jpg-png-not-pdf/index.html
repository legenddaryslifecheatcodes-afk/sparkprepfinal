Why you get “Cover file must be a PDF”

A JPG or PNG is just a picture. A print cover file also has to carry its exact size in inches, bleed and print color. That's why paperback cover uploads at KDP and IngramSpark expect a PDF.

Picture files bring other problems too: PNG and most JPGs are in screen color (RGB), and a picture saved for the web is usually far below 300 DPI at full cover size.

How to fix “Cover file must be a PDF” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Check your picture is at least 300 DPI at the full cover size (width in inches x 300 = pixels needed).
  2. Open it in a design app and make a document at your printer's exact full cover size, including bleed.
  3. Place the picture so it fills the whole page edge to edge.
  4. Export as a PDF, using PDF/X-1a:2001 and CMYK if your app offers them.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.

The most common mistake: Taking a cover picture saved for the web or social media and blowing it up to full print size.

How to check it's fixed before you upload again

  • The file you upload ends in .pdf and opens at your printer's exact full cover size with bleed.
  • The picture's pixel width is at least 300 times the cover width in inches.
  • Zoomed to 200%, text and edges are sharp, not blurry or blocky.

Quick answers

Can I upload a JPG cover to KDP for a paperback?

No. KDP's paperback cover upload expects a PDF. Turn the picture into a PDF at the exact full cover size first.

Does saving a JPG as a PDF fix it?

Only if the PDF is the exact full cover size with bleed, and the picture has enough resolution at that size.
