How to fix cover text that's too close to the edge

Updated October 28, 2026 · Amazon KDP · IngramSpark · Barnes & Noble Press · Lulu

If your title runs right up to the edge, the printer's blade can clip it. Here's how much room to leave.

Why you get “Text is too close to the trim edge and may be cut off”

Covers are printed bigger and then trimmed. The cut can move a tiny bit, so anything right at the edge is at risk. Amazon KDP asks you to keep text at least 0.125" inside the trim line. SparkPrep recommends 0.25" or more so nothing important ever gets close.

Spine text needs room too: it shouldn't touch the folds between the spine and the covers.

How to fix “Text is too close to the trim edge and may be cut off” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Open your original cover file and turn on the trim and bleed guides.
  2. Find any text, logo or face closer than 0.25" to the trim line (0.125" is the bare minimum at KDP).
  3. Move or shrink it so it sits comfortably inside. On the spine, leave space on both sides of the text.
  4. Re-export as PDF/X-1a:2001 with the bleed included.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.

Quick answers

How far from the edge should text be on a book cover?

Amazon KDP's minimum is 0.125" inside the trim line. Leaving 0.25" or more is safer.

Can background art go to the edge?

Yes. Background art should go past the edge into the bleed. Only text and important details need to stay inside.
