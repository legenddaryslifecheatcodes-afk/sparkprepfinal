How to fix a duplicate or missing barcode on your back cover

Updated November 14, 2026 · Amazon KDP · IngramSpark

One barcode belongs on the back cover. Too many authors end up with two, or none.

Cover has a barcode in the barcode area, or no barcode where one is needed

Why did your file get rejected?

Amazon KDP can place a barcode for you on the back cover. If your designer already put one in and you don't tell KDP your cover includes a barcode, you can end up with a second one printed over your art. KDP's barcode area is 2" x 1.2" in the lower right of the back cover.

IngramSpark needs a barcode on the back cover too. Its barcode area is 1.75" x 1". If your file has none, or it doesn't match your ISBN, the cover is rejected.

How to fix it manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Decide who adds the barcode: you (in your cover file) or your printer.
  2. If it's in your file, make sure it encodes your book's exact ISBN and sits in the back cover's lower right, inside the safe area.
  3. Keep that barcode area clear of text and busy art.
  4. On KDP, tick that your cover already includes a barcode if it does, so it isn't added twice.
  5. Re-upload the cover and check the printer's preview or proof for exactly one barcode.

Quick answers

Does KDP add a barcode for me?

KDP can place one on the back cover. If your cover already has a barcode, tell KDP so it doesn't add a second.

How big is the barcode area?

2" x 1.2" on KDP and 1.75" x 1" on IngramSpark, in the lower right of the back cover.
