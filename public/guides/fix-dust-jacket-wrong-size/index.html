How to fix a hardcover dust jacket that's the wrong size

Updated November 13, 2026 · IngramSpark · Barnes & Noble Press

A dust jacket is the paper cover that wraps around a hardcover and tucks inside with flaps. It's much wider than any other cover.

How big is a hardcover dust jacket? A dust jacket has a back flap, fold, back panel, spine, front panel, fold and front flap. Flaps are 3.25" and folds 0.25", plus 0.125" bleed. For a 6" x 9" book, IngramSpark's template is 20.438" x 9.5" and Barnes & Noble Press's is 20.451" x 9.5". KDP and Lulu don't offer dust jackets.

Why you get “Dust jacket file does not match the jacket template size”

A dust jacket has seven parts from left to right: back flap, fold, back panel, spine, front panel, fold, front flap. Each flap is 3.25" and each fold is 0.25", plus 0.125" bleed around the outside.

The panels are bigger than your trim because the jacket covers the boards. For a 6" x 9" book:

  • IngramSpark: each panel is the trim + 0.4375" wide and + 0.25" tall. Their own template for a 0.313" spine is 20.438" x 9.5".
  • Barnes & Noble Press: same layout, but each panel is about trim + 0.4445" wide. Their own template for a 0.31" spine is 20.451" x 9.5".

Amazon KDP and Lulu don't offer a dust jacket binding at all, so there is no jacket file to make for them.

How to fix “Dust jacket file does not match the jacket template size” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Download your printer's own dust jacket template for your exact trim size, page count and paper.
  2. Set your cover document to the template's full size.
  3. Line up the back flap, back panel, spine, front panel and front flap with the template, and keep text off the 0.25" folds.
  4. Hide the template layer, flatten, and export as PDF/X-1a:2001.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.

The most common mistake: Making the jacket panels the same size as the book's trim. They have to be bigger because the jacket covers the boards.

How to check it's fixed before you upload again

  • Your file's full size matches your own printer's jacket template, not the other printer's.
  • No text or faces sit on the 0.25" folds.
  • Flap text stays well inside each 3.25" flap.

Quick answers

How wide are dust jacket flaps?

3.25" each at IngramSpark and Barnes & Noble Press, with a 0.25" fold between each flap and its panel.

Can I print a dust jacket through KDP?

No. KDP's hardcover is case laminate only, so it has no dust jacket option.
