How to fix margin errors on Lulu and Barnes & Noble Press

Updated November 9, 2026 · Lulu · Barnes & Noble Press

Lulu and Barnes & Noble Press both want a clear half inch around your text. Here's how to give it to them.

What margins do Lulu and Barnes & Noble Press need? Both want everything on the page, including page numbers and headers, at least 0.5" from every trim edge. Pages are trimmed after printing and the inside edge curves into the spine, so text closer than that can be cut off or lost. Thick books need more space on the inside edge.

Why you get “Content is outside the safety margin”

Pages are trimmed after printing and the inside edge curves into the spine. To keep words from being cut or lost, Lulu and Barnes & Noble Press want everything, including page numbers and headers, at least 0.5" from every trim edge.

The usual cause is a manuscript set up with Word's default page and margins, or pictures and page numbers pushed toward the edge.

How to fix “Content is outside the safety margin” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Open your manuscript in Word (or InDesign).
  2. Set the page size to your exact trim size (Layout → Size).
  3. Set every margin to at least 0.5" (more on the inside for thick books), and use "Mirror margins".
  4. Check page numbers, headers and pictures sit inside the margins, then re-export as PDF/X-1a:2001 with fonts embedded.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.

The most common mistake: Fixing the body text margins but leaving page numbers and headers in Word's header and footer area, close to the edge.

How to check it's fixed before you upload again

  • Every page is your exact trim size.
  • Body text, page numbers, headers and pictures all sit at least 0.5" from every edge.
  • Mirror margins are on, with the wider margin on the spine side.
