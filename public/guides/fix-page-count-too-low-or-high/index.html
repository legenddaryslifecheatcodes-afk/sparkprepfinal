How to fix "page count is below the minimum" (or over the maximum)

Why you get “Page count is below the minimum (or above the maximum) for this format”

Binding machines need enough pages to glue a spine, and can only hold so many. The limits SparkPrep uses for paperbacks:

PrinterFewest pagesMost pages
Amazon KDP24828
IngramSpark181,200
Barnes & Noble Press48800
Lulu32800

Limits can change with trim size, paper and color, so always confirm on your printer's own calculator. Remember the printer counts every page in the PDF, blank pages included.

How to fix “Page count is below the minimum (or above the maximum) for this format” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Export your interior as a PDF and read the real page count there.
  2. Compare it with your printer's limits for your trim size and paper.
  3. Too few pages: add real front and back matter (title page, copyright page, table of contents, about the author) or choose a smaller trim size. Too many: choose a bigger trim size or split the book.
  4. Re-export the PDF and update the page count (and your cover's spine) in your book setup.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.
