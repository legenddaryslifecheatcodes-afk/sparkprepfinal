How to fix "spine text not allowed" on a thin book

Updated October 28, 2026 · Amazon KDP · IngramSpark

Thin books have thin spines, and printers won't print words on a spine that's too narrow to hold them.

The error: “Spine text is not allowed for books under the minimum page count”

Why you get “Spine text is not allowed for books under the minimum page count”

A spine is only as wide as your pages are thick. On a thin book, the spine might be a few millimeters wide. The printer's fold can shift a tiny bit, so words on a very thin spine can wrap onto the front or back cover.

That's why printers set a minimum: Amazon KDP only allows spine text on books with more than 79 pages, and IngramSpark doesn't allow spine text on paperbacks under 48 pages. If your book is under the line and the spine has words on it, the cover is rejected.

How to fix “Spine text is not allowed for books under the minimum page count” manually (the hard way)

If you want to try adjusting the files yourself, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Open your original cover file.
  2. Check your final page count against the rule: more than 79 pages for KDP, at least 48 for IngramSpark paperbacks.
  3. If you're under it, delete the title, author name and any logo from the spine. Keep just the background color or art there.
  4. Re-export as PDF/X-1a:2001 with the bleed included.
  5. Re-upload it to your printer and wait for the file review again. If it fails, you start over.
