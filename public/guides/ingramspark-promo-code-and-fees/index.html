IngramSpark promo code: do you still need one in 2026?

Updated November 16, 2026 · IngramSpark, for first-time self-publishers

For years, authors hunted for IngramSpark promo codes to skip the setup fee. That fee is gone. Here is what IngramSpark charges now, in plain English, and the cost nobody puts in the price list: a rejected file.

Do you need an IngramSpark promo code in 2026? No. IngramSpark says setting up a book is free, and fixing and re-uploading files has been free since February 1, 2026. Bulk-print discounts start at 100 copies and apply automatically. IngramSpark charges a 1.875% market access fee on books sold through its network, and printing is taken out of each sale.

Do you need an IngramSpark promo code?

No. IngramSpark says title setup is free, and since February 1, 2026 revisions (uploading a fixed file) are free too. Bulk-order discounts, starting at 100 copies, apply on their own with no promo code needed (IngramSpark's own help page).

Be careful with websites that still sell or list "IngramSpark coupon codes." Old codes were for the setup fee that no longer exists.

What IngramSpark actually costs in 2026

  • Setting up a book: free.
  • Fixing and re-uploading files: free since February 1, 2026.
  • Market access fee: 1.875%, taken from books sold through IngramSpark's network of over 45,000 stores, libraries and schools.
  • Printing: taken out of each sale. It depends on your trim size, page count, paper and color. IngramSpark's calculator shows your exact number.
  • Your ISBN: IngramSpark does not give you a free one in the US. You buy it from Bowker.

Prices change, so always check ingramspark.com/pricing before you upload.

The cost that isn't on the price list

Free uploads don't mean free time. IngramSpark's checker is strict. It rejects files for things like:

  • Cover art that doesn't run past the trim line (bleed).
  • Too much ink in dark areas, over the 240% limit.
  • Text too close to the page edge (safety area).
  • A spine that's the wrong width for your page count and paper.
  • Fonts that aren't built into the PDF.

Each rejection means fixing the file, uploading again and waiting for another review. Many first-time authors lose days or weeks this way.

Should you use IngramSpark, Amazon KDP, or both?

Many authors use both: Amazon KDP for Amazon, and IngramSpark to reach bookstores and libraries. Each has its own file rules, so a file that passes one can still fail the other. See KDP vs IngramSpark vs Lulu file rules.
