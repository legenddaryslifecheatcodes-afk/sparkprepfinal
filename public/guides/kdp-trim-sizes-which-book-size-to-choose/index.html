KDP trim sizes: which size should your first book be?

Updated November 19, 2026 · Amazon KDP paperback and hardcover

Trim size is the size of your finished book. It is the first choice you make, and it changes your page count, your printing cost and your cover size. Here is how to pick one without overthinking it.

What is the most common book size on KDP? 6 x 9 inches is the most common paperback size in the U.S., according to Amazon KDP. Novels also often use 5 x 8 or 5.5 x 8.5 inches. KDP treats books wider than 6.12 inches or taller than 9 inches as large trim sizes, which cost more to print.

What trim size means

Trim size is your book's width and height after the printer cuts the pages. It's written width first: a 6 x 9 book is 6 inches wide and 9 inches tall. You set it as the page size in Word (or whatever you write in) before you format anything else.

These suit workbooks, cookbooks, journals, photo books and children's picture books. The square and 8.5 x 11 sizes top out at 590 pages on white paper.

Hardcover sizes

KDP hardcovers come in 5.5 x 8.5, 6 x 9, 6.14 x 9.21, 7 x 10 and 8.25 x 11 inches, with 75 to 550 pages. If you want a hardcover later, picking one of these sizes for your paperback keeps both editions the same.

All sizes and page ranges from KDP's Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins page, checked November 2026.

How to choose in 3 questions

  1. What are similar books in your genre? Pull three off a shelf (or check the "Product details" on Amazon) and match them.
  2. Do you want bookstores too? Stick to a size IngramSpark also prints. 5 x 8, 5.5 x 8.5 and 6 x 9 are safe everywhere.
  3. Is your book short? A smaller size gives more pages and a thicker spine. Spine text is only allowed on KDP books with more than 79 pages.

Bleed changes your page size

If any picture or color runs to the edge of the page, you need bleed: add 0.125 inch to the width and 0.25 inch to the height. A 6 x 9 book with bleed is set up at 6.125 x 9.25. Your margins depend on page count too. See the full trim size and margin chart for KDP and IngramSpark, or read what to do if your page size doesn't match.

Quick answers

What size is a standard paperback novel?

Common novel sizes are 5 x 8, 5.25 x 8, 5.5 x 8.5 and 6 x 9 inches. 6 x 9 is the most common U.S. paperback size overall, according to KDP.

Can I change my trim size after publishing on KDP?

Not on the same book. Trim size is locked once a paperback is published. To change it, you create a new edition, which also needs a new cover file sized for the new trim.

What is a large trim size on KDP?

Any book wider than 6.12 inches or taller than 9 inches. Large trim sizes have higher printing costs on KDP.
