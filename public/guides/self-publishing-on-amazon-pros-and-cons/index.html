Self-publishing on Amazon: the pros and cons for your first book

Updated November 16, 2026 · Amazon KDP, for first-time self-publishers

Amazon KDP is where most first-time authors start. It's free and fast, but it has rules and trade-offs nobody explains up front. Here are the honest pros and cons.

What are the pros and cons of self-publishing on Amazon? Pros: it's free, fast (about 3 business days), reaches the most readers and pays 70% on ebooks priced $2.99 to $9.99. Cons: paperbacks under $9.99 earn 50% instead of 60%, KDP Select ebooks are Amazon-only for 90 days, bookstores rarely order KDP books, and strict file rules cause rejections.

The pros

  • It's free to publish. No setup fee for an ebook, paperback or hardcover. Printing is taken out of each sale, so you don't pay up front.
  • Huge reach. Your book is sold on Amazon, where many readers already shop for books.
  • Good ebook royalties. You can earn 70% on ebooks priced from $2.99 to $9.99 (KDP ebook royalties). Outside that range it's 35%.
  • Fast. Books usually go live within about 3 business days after you hit publish.
  • Print on demand. Amazon prints a copy only when someone buys it. No boxes of books in your garage.

The cons

  • Lower royalties on cheap paperbacks. Since June 10, 2025, KDP pays 60% on print books priced at $9.99 or more, but only 50% below that, minus printing (KDP paperback royalty).
  • Exclusivity if you join KDP Select. Select puts your ebook in Kindle Unlimited, but you can't sell that ebook anywhere else for 90 days at a time. It's optional.
  • Bookstores rarely stock KDP books. Most bookstores and libraries order through IngramSpark, which is why many authors use both.
  • Strict file rules. KDP checks page size, margins, bleed and picture sharpness. If one thing is off, your file is sent back and you fix it and wait again.
  • Amazon controls the store. Amazon can discount your book, and its rules can change, as the 2025 royalty change showed.

Is self-publishing on Amazon worth it?

For most first-time authors, yes. It costs nothing to start and reaches the most readers. Price your paperback at $9.99 or more if you can, to get the 60% rate. Decide on KDP Select only for your ebook, and only if you're fine selling it on Amazon alone.

Quick answers

Is self-publishing on Amazon free?

Yes. Amazon KDP charges nothing to publish an ebook, paperback or hardcover. Printing is taken out of each sale.

How much does Amazon pay authors?

KDP pays 70% on ebooks priced $2.99 to $9.99 and 35% otherwise. Since June 10, 2025, print books earn 60% at $9.99 or more and 50% below that, minus printing.

What are the downsides of self-publishing on Amazon?

Lower royalties on paperbacks under $9.99, 90-day ebook exclusivity if you join KDP Select, few bookstore orders, strict file rules that cause rejections, and Amazon controls pricing and policies.
