SparkPrep vs hiring on Reedsy: what it really costs and how long it takes

Updated October 28, 2026 · For first-time and experienced self-publishers

Reedsy is a marketplace of vetted publishing professionals, and it's good at what it does. But if what you need is a cover and interior that pass KDP or IngramSpark, you may be paying designer prices for a job that takes SparkPrep minutes.

The real numbers

These are Reedsy's own published figures, not ours:

Service on ReedsyReedsy's figure
Professional book coverMedian about $930; about half of projects cost $630 to $1,200
Interior design / typesettingAbout $800 on average; about half cost $475 to $1,275
Cover and interior togetherRoughly $1,100 to $2,475 (adding Reedsy's two ranges above)
Whole self-publishing budget (with editing)$2,940 to $5,660

Sources: Reedsy, cost to self-publish and book cover design cost, checked October 28, 2026.

Speed

With a freelancer you send a brief, wait for a slot in their schedule, review drafts and ask for changes. That takes days or weeks, and if the printer rejects the file, you go back and wait again.

SparkPrep works the moment you upload. It checks your cover and every page against your printer's own rules, explains every problem in plain English and fixes it, in minutes. No back and forth, no waiting.

When Reedsy is the right call

  • You want original illustrated artwork drawn for your cover.
  • You need a human editor for your writing.

For everything else, including designing a cover from a template, formatting your pages, and making sure both pass the printer, SparkPrep does it for one flat price per book. You can even bring a Reedsy designer's file to SparkPrep to check it before you upload.
