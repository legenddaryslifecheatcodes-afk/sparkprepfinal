Quick answers

How much does Vellum cost?

Vellum costs $199.99 for ebooks only and $249.99 with paperback formatting, and it runs only on Mac.

Does Atticus or Vellum make book covers?

No. Both format the inside of your book. You need a separate tool or designer for the full wrap cover. SparkPrep's Cover Studio designs the front, spine and back sized to your printer.
