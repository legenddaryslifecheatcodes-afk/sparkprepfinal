What is an ISBN number? A plain-English guide for your first book

Updated November 18, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Bowker (U.S.)

ISBN is one of the first words you'll see when you set up your book on Amazon KDP or IngramSpark. It's just your book's ID number. Here is what it is, what the numbers mean, and how to get one.

What is an ISBN number? An ISBN (International Standard Book Number) is a 13-digit number that identifies one book in one format, so stores and libraries order the right one. It is printed above the barcode on the back cover and on the copyright page. Paperback, hardcover and ebook each need their own. KDP and IngramSpark give free ones.

What ISBN stands for

ISBN stands for International Standard Book Number. Think of it like a license plate for your book. Bookstores, libraries and online shops use it to find, order and sell the exact book you made.

  • Every ISBN made since January 1, 2007 has 13 digits. Older books may show a 10-digit one.
  • One ISBN = one book in one format. Your paperback, hardcover and ebook each get a different number.
  • An ISBN is not copyright. You own your writing with or without one.

What the 13 digits mean

An ISBN looks like 978-0-306-40615-7. It has five parts:

PartIn the exampleWhat it means
Prefix978Says "this is a book." It is always 978 or 979.
Group0The country or language area (0 and 1 are English-speaking countries).
Publisher306Who published the book. If you buy your own ISBN, this part is you.
Title40615Which book, and which format, from that publisher.
Check digit7A math check, so a typo in the number gets caught.

You never have to work these out yourself. The ISBN is given to you whole.

Where the ISBN goes on your book

  • Back cover: printed above the barcode, usually at the bottom right.
  • Copyright page: the page behind the title page, often listed by format ("Paperback ISBN: ...").
  • Your book details on KDP or IngramSpark, when you set the book up.

All three must be the same number. Look up the exact spot on the cover in where the barcode goes on a book cover, and get the full size of your back cover from the book cover size chart.

How to get an ISBN

  • Free from KDP: for Amazon paperbacks and hardcovers. It only works on Amazon.
  • Free from IngramSpark (U.S. authors): it only works with IngramSpark.
  • Buy your own from Bowker, the official U.S. ISBN agency: $125 for one, $295 for ten. Your own ISBN works everywhere and lists you as the publisher. Other countries have their own agency (Canada's are free).

Quick answers

What does ISBN stand for?

ISBN stands for International Standard Book Number. It is the 13-digit number that identifies a book and its format.

Where do I find the ISBN on a book?

On the back cover, printed just above the barcode, and on the copyright page inside the book.

Is an ISBN the same as a barcode?

No. The ISBN is the number. The barcode is the pattern of stripes that holds that number so a store scanner can read it.

Does a Kindle ebook need an ISBN?

No. Amazon gives Kindle ebooks their own number, called an ASIN. Other ebook stores may ask for one, and it must be different from your print ISBN.

Do I need a new ISBN for a second edition?

Yes, if the book changes a lot (new chapters, a new size or a new format). Fixing a few typos does not need a new ISBN.
