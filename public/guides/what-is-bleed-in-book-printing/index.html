What is bleed in printing? A simple explanation for your first book

Updated November 24, 2026 · Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, Barnes & Noble Press

Bleed is one of the first printing words new authors run into, usually in a rejection email. It sounds technical. It isn't. It's just a little extra picture around the edge, so nothing white shows when the paper is cut.

What is bleed?

Bleed is extra picture or color that runs past the edge of a printed page, so no thin white line shows after the printer cuts the paper. Amazon KDP and IngramSpark ask for 0.125 inch (3.2 mm) of bleed past each cut edge: top, bottom and outside. Every KDP cover needs bleed.

  • How much: 0.125 inch (3.2 mm) on every edge that gets cut.
  • Page size with bleed: add 0.125 inch to the width and 0.25 inch to the height (6 x 9 becomes 6.125 x 9.25).
  • Who needs it: every cover, plus any inside page where a picture or color touches the edge.
  • Text-only novels: no bleed needed inside.

Why printers need it

Printers don't print on paper cut to your book's exact size. They print on bigger sheets, then cut them down. The blade can move a tiny bit. If your picture stops exactly at the edge, that tiny shift leaves a thin white line. Bleed is the safety cushion: you extend the picture past the cut, so the cut always lands on picture.

How much bleed?

0.125 inch (3.2 mm) on each side that is cut. For inside pages, that's the top, bottom and outside edge, not the spine side. So you add 0.125 inch to the width and 0.25 inch to the height.

Does my book need bleed?

  • Your cover: always. Every KDP and IngramSpark cover needs bleed on all four outside edges.
  • Inside pages with only text: no. Most novels don't need bleed inside.
  • Inside pages with a picture or color that touches the edge: yes, and if even one page needs it, the whole file must be set up with bleed.

Bleed's partner: the safe zone

Bleed is for pictures. Words need the opposite: keep them well inside the edge, so the cut never clips them. On KDP, keep text at least 0.375 inch from the edge on pages with bleed. Read: text too close to the edge.

Quick answers

How much bleed does KDP need?

0.125 inch (3.2 mm) past each trimmed edge: top, bottom and outside. That makes a 6 x 9 book's page size 6.125 x 9.25 inches.

Do novels need bleed?

Usually only on the cover. If the inside pages are just text with white margins, the interior doesn't need bleed.

What happens if I don't add bleed?

The printer may reject the file, or you may get a thin white line along the edge of the printed cover or picture pages.
