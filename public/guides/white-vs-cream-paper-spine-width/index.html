White vs cream paper: how your paper choice changes your spine

Updated November 1, 2026 · Amazon KDP · IngramSpark

Switching from white to cream paper feels like a small choice. It changes your cover size.

1. The error: “Spine width does not match the page count and paper selected”

Spine width does not match the page count and paper selected

Paraphrased. This is what you get when a cover built for white paper is uploaded for a cream-paper book.

2. Why the printer rejected it

Every page adds thickness, and cream paper is thicker than white. So the same 300-page book has a different spine on each paper:

KDP paperSpine per page300 pages
White0.002252"0.676"
Cream0.0025"0.750"
Premium color0.002347"0.704"

IngramSpark measures paper in pages per inch: about 512 for white 50#, 444 for creme 50#, 400 for groundwood and 377 for 70# color. Each printer's own numbers only work for that printer.

3. The do-it-yourself fix

Before you design

  1. Pick your paper first, then get the cover size.
  2. Use the spine width calculator or the printer's own template for that paper.

If you already switched paper

  1. Get a new template for the new paper and page count.
  2. Widen the cover by the difference, move the front panel over, and re-center the spine text.

Quick answers

Is cream paper thicker than white?

Yes. On KDP a cream page adds 0.0025 inch to the spine and a white page adds 0.002252 inch, so cream books have wider spines.

Can I use KDP's spine width for IngramSpark?

No. Each printer's paper is different, so always use that printer's own numbers or template.
