How to self publish a book: 7 simple steps

Never published before? Here is the whole process in plain words, from finished manuscript to a book for sale on Amazon and in bookstores.

How do I self publish a book? Finish and edit your book, pick your trim size, format the inside pages as a PDF, make a full wrap cover sized to your page count, check both files against your printer's rules, then upload them free to Amazon KDP or IngramSpark. Order a proof copy, then publish.

The 7 steps

Step 1: Finish your book and get it edited

Write it all the way through, then fix it up. Have at least one other person read it. Run a spell check and use our free manuscript checklist.

Step 2: Pick your book size (trim size)

Trim size is how big your printed book will be. Many novels are 6 x 9 inches or 5.5 x 8.5 inches. Pick it before you set up your pages, because changing it later means redoing everything.

Step 3: Format the inside of your book

Formatting means getting your pages ready to print: page size, margins (including the inside margin, called the gutter), page numbers, chapter starts and fonts. You can do it in Word, Google Docs, Kindle Create or our free Interior Studio. Save it as a PDF with every font embedded. See book formatting.

Step 4: Make your cover

A print cover is one file: back cover, spine and front cover together, called a full wrap cover. The spine width depends on your page count and paper, so finish your pages first. Use the free spine width calculator or Cover Studio.

Step 5: Check your whole book before you upload

This is the step most first-time authors skip, and it is why so many books get sent back. Printers check things you can't see on screen: bleed, the safe zone, embedded fonts, 300 DPI pictures, CMYK color and the cover size. A preflight check catches them first.

Step 6: Upload to Amazon KDP and IngramSpark

Amazon KDP (kdp.amazon.com) is free to upload and sells on Amazon. It can give you a free ISBN. IngramSpark (ingramspark.com) gets your book into bookstores and libraries and works best with your own ISBN. Many authors use both.

Step 7: Order a proof copy, then publish

Order one printed copy and look it over before you go live. KDP says it takes about 3 business days for a book to go live after you submit it.

How much does it cost to self publish a book?

Uploading to Amazon KDP is free. Amazon takes its share and the printing cost when a copy sells. Where the money goes is getting your files ready:

  • Do it yourself: free, but you risk rejections and weeks of trial and error.
  • Hire freelancers: Reedsy's figures put a professional cover at a median of about $930 and interior formatting at about $800, roughly $1,730 for both.

Why do self-published books get rejected?

Almost always for one of these file problems:

  1. The cover is the wrong size, usually because the spine doesn't match the page count.
  2. Text sits outside the safe zone, too close to the edge.
  3. Fonts aren't embedded in the PDF.
  4. Pictures are under 300 DPI.
  5. The page size doesn't match the trim size you picked.

Each one has a step-by-step fix guide.

Common questions

Is it free to self publish a book on Amazon?

Yes. Uploading to Amazon KDP is free. Amazon takes its share and the printing cost for paperbacks when a copy sells.

Do I need an ISBN to self publish?

Not for Amazon. KDP can give you a free ISBN. For IngramSpark and bookstores it is best to buy your own, which in the US comes from Bowker.

How long does it take to self publish a book?

Once your book is written and edited, you can be ready to upload in a day or two if your files pass the printer's checks. KDP says about 3 business days to go live. Rejections and fixes are what stretch it into weeks.

What is the easiest way to self publish a book?
