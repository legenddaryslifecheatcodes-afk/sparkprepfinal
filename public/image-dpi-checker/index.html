Why 300 DPI matters for print

DPI (dots per inch) is how many pixels of your picture land in each printed inch. Screens look fine at 72 to 150, but paper shows every missing pixel. Printers ask for 300 DPI at the size it prints. A picture that looks sharp on your phone can still print blurry on a cover.

The "DPI" number in your file doesn't matter. Pixels do.

A photo can say "300 DPI" in its settings and still be too small. What counts is the number of pixels compared to the printed size. To print 6" wide at 300 DPI you need 6 x 300 = 1,800 pixels across. This checker does that math for you.

How this PDF DPI checker works

Drop your PDF into the checker at the top of this page. It finds every picture on every page and divides each picture's pixels by the size it is placed on the page. A 1,200-pixel-wide photo placed 4 inches wide is 300 DPI; the same photo stretched to 6 inches is only 200 DPI. That is the number KDP and IngramSpark care about, not the "DPI" saved in the picture's settings.

Other ways to check the DPI of a PDF

In Adobe Acrobat Pro (paid)

  1. Open your PDF in Acrobat Pro and open Preflight (under Print Production).
  2. Under PDF analysis, pick "List page objects, grouped by type of object" and click Analyze.
  3. In the results, open the Images list. Each picture shows its resolution in ppi (the same idea as DPI here).

In free Adobe Reader, Mac Preview or Windows

The free Adobe Acrobat Reader, Mac Preview and the Windows file viewer can't show the DPI of the pictures inside a PDF. Preview shows the page size, not the picture quality. That is why this checker exists: open it in any browser, on a Mac or a PC, and drop in the file.

What to do if your picture is too small

  • Find the original, full-size file (not one saved from email, a website or social media, which shrink pictures).
  • Buy or download the largest size from the stock photo site.
  • Print it smaller. The checker shows the biggest size that still reaches 300 DPI.
  • Simply changing the DPI number in Photoshop without adding pixels does not fix it.

Quick answers

Can I check the DPI of a PDF in Adobe Reader?

No. The free Adobe Acrobat Reader does not show the DPI of pictures inside a PDF. Adobe Acrobat Pro can, with its Preflight tool. This free checker shows it without Acrobat Pro.

How do I check the DPI of a PDF on a Mac?

Mac Preview shows page size but not the DPI of the pictures inside. Open this checker in Safari or Chrome and drop in the PDF. The file never leaves your computer.

What DPI should a PDF be for printing a book?

300 DPI at the size each picture prints. Amazon KDP and IngramSpark both ask for 300 DPI. Pictures under about 200 DPI can print soft or blurry.

What DPI does KDP require?

KDP recommends 300 DPI for images in print books and warns about images below that in its previewer.

How do I check the DPI of an image?

Divide the picture's width in pixels by the width in inches it will print. 1,800 pixels printed 6 inches wide is 300 DPI. This free checker does it for you.

How do I check the resolution of a PDF?

Drop your PDF into the checker above. It lists every picture on every page with its real DPI at the size it prints, and flags anything under 300 DPI. It's free and nothing is uploaded.

Does this tool upload my picture?

No. The picture is read in your own browser and never leaves your device.
