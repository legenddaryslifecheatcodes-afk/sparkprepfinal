Why did KDP or IngramSpark reject my book file?

Most rejections come from a few file problems: art that doesn't reach past the trim edge (bleed), text too close to the edge, the wrong spine width for your page count, blurry pictures under 300 DPI, screen colors (RGB) instead of print colors, too much ink in dark areas, or fonts that aren't built into the PDF.

How much does it cost to hire someone to format a book for print?

Reedsy reports a median of about $930 for a professional cover and an average of about $800 for interior formatting, so about $1,730 for both. Newer designers often charge about $300 to $500 for a cover.

How long does it take to get a book print-ready?

With freelancers it often takes several weeks: finding someone, waiting for their schedule and going through rounds of changes. Then KDP says it takes about 3 business days for a book, or each fix, to go live. SparkPrep checks and fixes your files in minutes.

Do I need a preflight check if I made my cover in Canva or my book in Word?

Yes. Canva and Word don't check printer rules. A file can look perfect on screen and still have no bleed, screen colors or the wrong spine width. A preflight check catches these before you upload.

Does SparkPrep work for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble and Lulu?

Yes. SparkPrep checks your files against each printer's own rules: Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press and Lulu.
