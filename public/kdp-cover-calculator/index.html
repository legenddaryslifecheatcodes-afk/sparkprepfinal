Why use a KDP cover size calculator?

KDP rejects any cover PDF that is even slightly the wrong size. Your cover's width depends on your trim size, page count and paper type, so guessing is risky. This calculator uses KDP's own formula to give you the exact width, height and spine in seconds, plus a free 300 DPI template to design on.

How KDP works out your cover size

Amazon KDP prints your paperback cover as one piece that wraps around the book: back cover, spine and front cover, plus a little extra on every edge (the bleed) that gets trimmed off. KDP's formula is:

Cover width = 0.125" bleed + back cover width + spine width + front cover width + 0.125" bleed

Cover height = 0.125" bleed + trim height + 0.125" bleed

So a 6" x 9" book is always 9.25" tall, and 12.25" wide plus the spine.

Cream paper is thicker than white, so a cover built for white paper but printed on cream comes out with a spine that's too narrow, one of the most common reasons a cover gets rejected or the spine art wraps onto the front.

Spine text rules

  • KDP only prints spine text on books with more than 79 pages.
  • Keep spine text at least 0.0625" away from each fold line. KDP allows that much movement when it folds the cover.

Other things that get KDP covers rejected

  • No bleed: background art must reach all the way to the outer edge of the template, not stop at the trim line.
  • Text too close to the edge: titles or names sitting in the trim zone get cut off.
  • Low-resolution images: photos under 300 DPI print blurry.
  • Wrong file size: a cover PDF that doesn't match the exact size above is rejected automatically.
