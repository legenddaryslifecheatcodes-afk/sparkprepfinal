Why formatters use it

A rejected file usually means an hour or two of unpaid work and an unhappy client. One saved redo a month pays for the plan. And a branded print-ready report makes a one-person studio look like a full prepress house.

Questions

Will my clients see SparkPrep? No. Your name and logo go on the reports. We never contact your clients.

What counts as a book? One check and fix of the interior and cover for one title. Cover design and editing are not included.

Are my clients' files safe? Files are never sold or shared and never used to train AI. See our privacy page.
