Privacy · Updated November 9, 2026

Your book stays yours

Writers and editors trust us with unpublished work. Here is exactly what happens to your files, in plain English.

The short version

  • We never sell or share your book files with anyone.
  • We never use your files to train AI.
  • You can delete your projects at any time from your dashboard.
  • Your original file never changes. It stays on your own computer. SparkPrep works on a copy.

Your book files

When you upload a cover or interior file, we keep it in your account only so you can see your audit report and use your repaired, print-ready files. Nobody else can see it.

The audit only reads your file. It reports problems and changes nothing. Repairs are a separate step that you choose to run, and every change is listed in your report so you can check it before you export.

Deleting your files

Open your dashboard and delete any project whenever you want. To delete your whole account, email legenddaryslifecheatcodes@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will remove it.

Your account

To make an account we need your email address and a password. We use your email to sign you in and to send things about your account, like your audit results. We do not sell or rent your email address.

Payments

Payments go through Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us, and we never see or store it.

Our page counter

We count page visits with our own small counter so we know which pages help authors. It uses no cookies and no ads, and it does not follow you to other websites. It records the page, the site you came from and a daily visitor number that cannot be traced back to you.

Questions

Email legenddaryslifecheatcodes@gmail.com and Joshua Davis, SparkPrep's founder, will answer you directly.
