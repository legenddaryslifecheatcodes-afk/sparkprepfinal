What makes a great children's cover

Children's book covers are bright, round and playful. Big friendly fonts and happy colors catch a child's eye, and a parent's too.

  • Round, friendly fonts like Fredoka are easy for kids to read.
  • Bright primary colors catch the eye.
  • Leave room for your illustration. It's the star of a picture book.
  • Keep the title short and large.

Quick answers

What font is best for a children's book cover?

Round, friendly fonts like Fredoka or Nunito. They're easy to read and feel playful.

Can I put my own illustration on the cover?

Yes. Upload your art in Cover Studio and place it on any template. Make sure it's 300 DPI at print size.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
