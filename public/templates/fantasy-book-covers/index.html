What makes a great fantasy cover

Fantasy covers promise a world. Rich colors, ornate serif titles and a sense of scale tell readers this is an adventure worth hundreds of pages.

  • Classic, carved-looking serif fonts like Cinzel feel epic.
  • Deep purples, golds and fire colors feel magical.
  • Emblems and symbols work well for series.
  • Keep the title clear even when the art is detailed.

Quick answers

What font do fantasy book covers use?

Ornate serif fonts like Cinzel or Cinzel Decorative are common. They look carved in stone, which suits epic fantasy.

Can I make a fantasy cover without custom art?

Yes. Strong color, a good title font and a simple emblem can look professional. You can add your own art or AI art in Cover Studio too.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
