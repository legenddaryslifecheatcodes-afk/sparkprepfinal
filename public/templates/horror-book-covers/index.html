What makes a great horror cover

Horror covers need to unsettle. Blood reds, deep shadows and a title font with an edge tell readers to brace themselves.

  • A distressed or dripping title font says horror right away.
  • Deep red and black are the classic pair.
  • Less is more: one creepy detail beats a crowded scene.
  • Keep the author name clean so it's still easy to read.

Quick answers

What font is used on horror book covers?

Distressed or dripping display fonts like Creepster for the title, with a plain bold font like Oswald for the author.

What colors are best for horror?

Black, deep red and off-white. They create tension and stay readable as small thumbnails.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
