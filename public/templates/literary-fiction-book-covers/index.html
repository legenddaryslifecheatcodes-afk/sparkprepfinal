What makes a great literary fiction cover

Literary fiction covers are calm and confident. Quiet colors, elegant serif type and lots of open space tell readers this book is about ideas and people.

  • Elegant serifs like DM Serif Display or Cormorant feel literary.
  • Muted earth tones and deep blues or greens work well.
  • White space is your friend.
  • A small, simple image can carry a lot of meaning.
