What makes a great nonfiction and business cover

Nonfiction and business covers sell a promise. A big, bold title, a clear subtitle that says what the reader gets, and clean colors look expert and trustworthy.

  • Make the title as big as you can. Readers scan nonfiction covers for the promise.
  • Bold sans-serif fonts like Archivo Black or Montserrat look modern and confident.
  • A clear subtitle does a lot of the selling.
  • Clean color blocks beat busy photos.

Quick answers

How do I design a business book cover?

Lead with a big, bold title and a clear subtitle that names the benefit. Use one or two strong colors and a clean font.

Do nonfiction covers need a subtitle?

Usually, yes. The subtitle tells readers exactly what they'll learn, which helps both sales and search.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
