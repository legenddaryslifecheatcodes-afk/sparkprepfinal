What makes a great poetry cover

Poetry covers are often quiet and beautiful. Soft colors, delicate type and lots of space let the title breathe, like a good poem.

  • Delicate serif or script fonts suit poetry.
  • Soft, muted colors feel thoughtful.
  • Minimal designs with one small image work beautifully.
  • Short collection titles look great set large.
