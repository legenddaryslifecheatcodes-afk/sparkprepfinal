What makes a great romance cover

Romance readers know what they want, and the cover tells them in a glance. Soft or rich colors, a flowing script title, and a clear feeling (sweet, steamy or funny) help your book find the right reader.

  • Script or elegant serif fonts signal romance right away.
  • Blush, plum and warm tones read sweet; deep reds and black read steamy.
  • Match the mood of your subgenre: rom-com covers are bright and playful.
  • Leave room for a tagline or series name.

Quick answers

What makes a good romance book cover?

A clear mood, a readable title and the colors your subgenre uses. Readers should know if it's sweet, steamy or funny before they read a word.

What font is best for a romance cover?

A flowing script like Great Vibes for the title, paired with a simple serif like Lora for the author name.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
