What makes a great science fiction cover

Sci-fi covers look clean, bright and futuristic. Glowing colors on dark space, or cool blues and whites, tell readers to expect technology, space or the future.

  • Geometric fonts like Orbitron or Space Grotesk feel futuristic.
  • Neon pinks and cyans on near-black feel like space or cyberpunk.
  • Cool icy blues feel like hard sci-fi or near-future.
  • Simple shapes and glows beat busy detail at thumbnail size.
