What makes a great self-help and faith cover

Self-help and faith covers feel hopeful and clear. Warm sunrise colors, calm blues and strong, simple titles tell readers this book will help them.

  • Warm gradients like sunset tones feel hopeful.
  • Keep the title short, bold and easy to read.
  • Use the subtitle to name the change the reader will make.
  • Simple symbols (a sunrise, a path, a light) work well.

Quick answers

What colors work for a self-help book cover?

Warm, hopeful colors like orange and gold, or calm blues. Bright and clean usually beats dark.

Can I use these templates for a Christian or devotional book?

Yes. The self-help and faith templates suit devotionals, and you can change every color, font and word.
