What makes a great thriller and mystery cover

Thriller and mystery readers decide fast. They look for dark backgrounds, one strong color, and a big, tight title they can read from across the room. Your cover has to say "danger" in under a second.

  • Use a heavy, condensed title font in all caps.
  • Keep to two or three colors: black or deep blue, plus one alarm color like red or gold.
  • One image (a figure, a door, a road) beats a busy collage.
  • Make the author name big if this is a series.

Quick answers

What colors work best on a thriller cover?

Dark backgrounds with one bright accent, like black with red, or navy with gold. High contrast makes the title readable as a small thumbnail.

What font should I use for a thriller book cover?

A bold, condensed sans-serif in capitals, like Bebas Neue or Oswald. Both are free and come with SparkPrep's Cover Studio.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
