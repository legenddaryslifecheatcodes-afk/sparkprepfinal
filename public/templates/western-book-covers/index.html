What makes a great western and historical cover

Western and historical covers feel weathered and timeless. Dusty earth tones and slab-serif "wanted poster" type put readers in another time and place right away.

  • Slab-serif fonts like Rye feel like the old West.
  • Dusty browns, tans and cream are the classic palette.
  • A lone figure, a horizon or a landmark sets the scene.
  • Historical fiction often uses period photos or paintings.

Quick answers

What font do western book covers use?

Slab-serif and "wanted poster" fonts like Rye, with a sturdy serif like Bitter for the author name.

Can I use these for historical fiction?

Yes. Swap in a period photo or painting and the earth-tone palettes work well.

Will these templates fit my book's spine?

Yes. Each template is built on your book's real cover size, so the spine matches your page count and paper for KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu.
