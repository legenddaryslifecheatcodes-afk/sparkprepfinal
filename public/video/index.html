What happens in the video

  • The rejection: the printer bounces the book file. This happens to a lot of first-time authors on Amazon KDP and IngramSpark.
  • The check: SparkPrep checks the cover and every page for size, bleed, margins, spine width, colors, ink limits, picture sharpness and fonts.
  • The fix: SparkPrep fixes what it safely can and exports print-ready files.

Common reasons a book file gets rejected

  • Cover is the wrong size
  • Text too close to the edge (margins)
  • Blurry pictures (under 300 DPI)
  • RGB vs CMYK colors
  • Too much ink (240% limit)
