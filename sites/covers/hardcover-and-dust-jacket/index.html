Hardcovers

Hardcover and dust jacket covers: what's different

A hardcover isn't a paperback cover printed bigger. The cover wraps around boards, bends at hinges and, with a dust jacket, folds into flaps. Each has its own no-go zones.

Quick answer

A hardcover case wrap is printed bigger than the book and wrapped around the boards, so it needs extra wrap area and clear hinge areas beside the spine. A dust jacket adds front and back flaps. A jacketed book can need three files: case, jacket and interior. Always build from your printer's hardcover template.

What's different from a paperback

  • Wrap area. A case cover is bigger than the book because it wraps around the edges of the boards. Art must run into the wrap; nothing important can sit there.
  • Hinges. Next to the spine, the cover bends where the board meets the spine. Keep text and faces out of the hinge.
  • Wider spine. Boards add thickness, so a hardcover spine is wider than the same book in paperback.
  • Flaps (jackets). Jacket flaps fold inside the cover. Text on the flaps must stay clear of the fold lines.

Mistakes that get hardcovers rejected

  • Reusing the paperback cover at hardcover size. The panels don't line up.
  • Text across the jacket fold (between front panel and flap). It ends up bent inside the book.
  • Missing a file. Uploading only the jacket when the printer also needs the case.
  • Template lines left in. Hardcover templates have many guide lines; every one must be deleted.

How to do it right

  1. Finish the interior and get the final page count.
  2. Download your printer's hardcover template for that trim, page count and paper. Use the case template for a printed case; the jacket template for a dust jacket.
  3. Design on the template, keeping text out of wrap, hinge and fold areas.
  4. Delete the template layer, export as PDF/X-1a and preflight.

Questions people ask

Does KDP offer dust jackets?

No. KDP hardcovers are case laminate, with the art printed on the cover itself. IngramSpark offers dust jackets.

Can I use my paperback cover for a hardcover?

Not as is. A hardcover needs wrap and hinge areas and a wider spine, so it must be rebuilt on the hardcover template.
