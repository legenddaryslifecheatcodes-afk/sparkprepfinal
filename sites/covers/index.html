Print covers, made simple

How big is a print book cover? (The formula)

An ebook cover is just the front. A print cover is one wide file: back cover, spine and front cover side by side, plus a little extra around the edge for trimming.

Quick answer

Full cover width = 0.125" bleed + book width + spine width + book width + 0.125" bleed. Full cover height = 0.125" + book height + 0.125". Spine width = page count x your printer's paper thickness. Example: a 6" x 9", 300-page KDP book on white paper is 12.926" x 9.25".

Worked example

A 6" x 9" paperback, 300 pages, white paper, printed by KDP:

  1. Spine = 300 x 0.002252" = 0.676"
  2. Width = 0.125 + 6 + 0.676 + 6 + 0.125 = 12.926"
  3. Height = 0.125 + 9 + 0.125 = 9.25"
  4. At 300 DPI that's about 3878 x 2775 pixels.

The same book at IngramSpark on 50# white paper has a spine of about 0.586" (300 ÷ 512), so its cover is about 12.836" wide. Same book, different cover. See the KDP chart and the IngramSpark chart.

The safe zone

Printers trim and fold with a little wiggle. Keep anything important (words, faces, logos) inside a safe zone:

  • At least 0.125" inside the trim line for KDP, and more is better. Many designers use 0.25".
  • At least 0.0625" (1/16") from each spine fold for spine text.
  • The barcode box on the back kept clear.

Order of work that saves redoing your cover

  1. Choose printer, trim size and paper.
  2. Finish the interior so you know the final page count.
  3. Get the spine width and full cover size for that printer.
  4. Download that printer's template, or start in a tool that's already sized.
  5. Design, then preflight before upload.

Questions people ask

What size is a 6x9 book cover with spine?

It depends on page count and paper. A 300-page 6x9 KDP paperback on white paper is 12.926 by 9.25 inches including bleed.

Is the bleed included in the cover size?

Yes. Printers want the uploaded cover at full size including 0.125 inch bleed on the outer edges.
