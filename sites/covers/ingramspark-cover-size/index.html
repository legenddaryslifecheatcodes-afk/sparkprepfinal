IngramSpark cover size and spine width

IngramSpark measures paper in pages per inch (PPI), not thickness per page. Divide your page count by your paper's PPI and you have the spine. Then build the cover around it.

Quick answer

IngramSpark spine width ≈ page count ÷ pages per inch (about 512 for 50# white, 444 for 50# creme, 400 for groundwood, 377 for 70# color). Full cover width = 0.25" + 2 x trim width + spine; height = trim height + 0.25". Always confirm with IngramSpark's own cover template.

What else IngramSpark checks on covers

  • Total ink under 240%. Dark covers fail this most. How to fix it.
  • Barcode area of 1.75" x 1" on the back left clear. Details.
  • No template guide lines left in the art. The template's colored lines must be deleted before export.
  • PDF/X-1a file with fonts embedded and no live transparency.
  • No spine text under 48 pages (paperback).

Moving a KDP cover to IngramSpark

  1. Generate IngramSpark's template for the same trim, page count and paper.
  2. Compare spine widths. On white paper, IngramSpark's is usually narrower than KDP's.
  3. Narrow the spine, slide the front cover in by the difference and re-center spine text.
  4. Check the barcode area (IngramSpark's is a different size and position from KDP's).
  5. Check total ink and export as PDF/X-1a.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate IngramSpark spine width?

Divide your page count by your paper's pages per inch, about 512 for 50 lb white and 444 for 50 lb creme. Then confirm with IngramSpark's cover template.

Is the IngramSpark spine the same as KDP?

No. Their papers differ, so the same book gets a different spine width at each printer.
