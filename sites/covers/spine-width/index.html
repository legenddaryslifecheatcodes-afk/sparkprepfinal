Spine widths

Book spine width chart (KDP and IngramSpark)

The spine is the only part of your cover that changes with your page count. Here it is for both big printers and every common paper, side by side.

Quick answer

KDP spine = pages x 0.002252" (white), 0.0025" (cream) or 0.002347" (premium color). IngramSpark spine ≈ pages ÷ 512 (50# white), 444 (50# creme), 400 (groundwood) or 377 (70# color). A 200-page book on KDP white paper has a 0.450" spine.

Allowed isn't the same as readable. Under about 0.25" (roughly 110 pages on KDP white), spine text has to be tiny. Many authors leave thin spines plain, or use just the title.

Spine text that fits

  • Leave at least 1/16" (0.0625") clear on each side, between the text and the fold.
  • So the tallest letters can be at most spine width minus 0.125".
  • Example: a 0.45" spine allows text about 0.325" tall, which is roughly 24 to 28 point type.

Questions people ask

How wide is the spine of a 200-page book?

On KDP white paper, 0.450 inch. On KDP cream, 0.500 inch. On IngramSpark 50 lb white, about 0.391 inch.

Does cream paper make a thicker book?

Yes. Cream pages are thicker, so the spine is wider for the same page count.
