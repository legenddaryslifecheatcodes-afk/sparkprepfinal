The going-wide trap

Why your book passed KDP but failed IngramSpark

It's one of the most common surprises in self-publishing. KDP printed your book happily, then IngramSpark sent the same files back. Nothing is wrong with you: the two printers simply use different rules.

Quick answer

IngramSpark is stricter than KDP in five places: 0.5" margins on every edge (KDP allows 0.25" outside), a 240% ink limit, a different spine width (different paper), a different barcode box, and a preference for PDF/X-1a files. Files built for KDP's minimums often fail one of these.

The five differences, in order of how often they bite

1. Margins

KDP lets text sit 0.25" from the outside edge. IngramSpark wants 0.5" from every edge, including page numbers. If you used KDP's minimums, IngramSpark will say your content "extends outside the safety area". Margin fix.

2. Ink

KDP doesn't publish a total ink number. IngramSpark rejects anything over 240%. Dark covers and black and white interiors saved in color are the usual culprits. Ink fix.

3. Spine width

The papers are different. A 300-page book on white paper has a spine of about 0.676" at KDP and about 0.586" at IngramSpark. The KDP cover is almost 0.1" too wide for IngramSpark. Spine chart.

4. Barcode

KDP adds its barcode in a 2" x 1.2" box if you leave the space. IngramSpark's template has its own barcode position and size. A KDP cover with a blank corner may need a barcode added for IngramSpark. Barcode rules.

5. File type

KDP accepts a normal print PDF. IngramSpark prefers PDF/X-1a, which rules out live transparency and RGB color. A Canva or Word PDF can fail on this alone. Flatten and convert.

One interior for both printers

  • Margins: 0.5" or more on every edge, plus KDP's inside margin for your page count if it's bigger.
  • Black and white: grayscale only. Color: CMYK under 240%.
  • Fonts embedded, pictures 300 DPI, PDF/X-1a.

Built this way, the same interior usually passes both.

Questions people ask

Can I use my KDP files on IngramSpark?

The interior often works if it already has 0.5 inch margins and stays under 240% ink. The cover almost always needs re-fitting to IngramSpark's spine width and template.

Why is IngramSpark stricter than KDP?

IngramSpark prints for bookstores and libraries worldwide on many presses, so it holds every file to one strict print standard.
