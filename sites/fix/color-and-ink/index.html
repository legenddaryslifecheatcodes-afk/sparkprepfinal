Fix: color and ink

How to fix color and ink problems

Screens make color with light (RGB). Printers use four inks (CMYK). Most color errors come from a file that's still in screen color, or from black made out of all four inks at once.

Quick answer

Convert color files to CMYK and keep total ink (C+M+Y+K) under 240% for IngramSpark. Make black text 100% K only. For a black and white book, export the interior as grayscale so no color or rich black is left.

Black and white interior: the fastest fix

Export it as grayscale. Grayscale uses black ink only, so it can never go over 100% ink and has no color in it.

  1. Acrobat Pro: Tools → Print Production → Convert Colors.
  2. Conversion profile: a gray profile such as Dot Gain 15% or Gray Gamma 2.2. Apply to all pages.
  3. Save as a new PDF and upload that one.

No Acrobat? In Word, set all text to Automatic/black, turn pictures to grayscale (Picture Format → Color → Grayscale) and remove blue hyperlinks (select all, Ctrl+Shift+F9 turns links into plain text). It reduces the problem, but Word can't make a true grayscale PDF.

Color covers and interiors: bring ink under 240%

  1. Find it: Acrobat Pro → Print Production → Output Preview → tick Total Area Coverage, set 240%. Problem areas light up.
  2. Black text and black shapes: make them C0 M0 Y0 K100, not a "rich" mix.
  3. Big black backgrounds: use a lighter rich black, for example C40 M30 Y30 K100 (200% total).
  4. Photos: convert to CMYK with a profile that has a low ink limit, then check Output Preview again.
  5. Export as PDF/X-1a.

Canva

Canva Pro's PDF Print download has a CMYK color profile option. Use it for covers. It doesn't let you set an ink limit, so dark Canva covers often still pass KDP but fail IngramSpark's 240% check. Check the downloaded file, or let SparkPrep measure it.

Expect colors to change a little

Bright screen colors (electric blue, neon green, hot pink) can't be made with ink. They print duller. Look at your cover in CMYK before you approve it, and order a proof.

Questions people ask

What is total ink coverage?

Cyan, magenta, yellow and black added together at one spot. 100% black alone is 100%. A rich black can be 300% or more.

Does KDP need CMYK?

KDP accepts RGB and converts it, but colors can shift. IngramSpark's PDF/X-1a requires CMYK or grayscale.
