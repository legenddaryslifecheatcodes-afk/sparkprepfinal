Fix: transparency

How to flatten transparency and layers

Drop shadows, soft glows, faded pictures and see-through shapes are all "transparency". On screen they look great. On a printing press they can turn into white boxes, hard edges or missing art.

Quick answer

To flatten a book PDF, export it as PDF/X-1a (which doesn't allow live transparency or layers), or use your app's flatten option: Canva's Flatten PDF box, InDesign's Flattener Preview set to High Resolution, or Acrobat Pro's Print Production → Flattener Preview.

Where transparency hides

  • Drop shadows behind titles and author names.
  • Glows and soft edges on pictures.
  • Shapes or text with opacity under 100%.
  • PNG pictures with see-through backgrounds (logos, mascots, cut-out photos).
  • Blend modes (multiply, screen, overlay) in Photoshop, Canva or Affinity.

Fix it in Canva

  1. Share → Download → PDF Print.
  2. Tick Flatten PDF.
  3. Download, then zoom in to 400% on every shadow and faded area to check nothing changed.

Fix it in InDesign

  1. Export → Adobe PDF (Print) → preset PDF/X-1a:2001. This flattens automatically.
  2. Under Advanced, set Transparency Flattener to High Resolution.
  3. Before exporting, Window → Output → Flattener Preview shows what will change.

Fix it in Affinity Publisher or Photo

  1. File → Export → PDF → Preset PDF/X-1a:2003.
  2. For a single cover image, Document → Flatten (Affinity Photo) before export also works.

Fix a finished PDF in Acrobat Pro

  1. Tools → Print Production → Flattener Preview.
  2. Choose "High Resolution", tick "Apply to all pages", then Apply.
  3. Save as a new file, then Print Production → Preflight to confirm "no transparency".

After flattening, check these

  • Thin white lines ("stitching") across pictures. They often only show on screen, but check at high zoom.
  • Text that got heavier or blurry. If text was turned into a picture, flatten again with text kept as text (InDesign: tick "Convert all text to outlines" off).
  • Color shifts where a shadow overlapped a photo.

Questions people ask

Does KDP accept transparency?

KDP recommends flattening. IngramSpark's preferred format, PDF/X-1a, doesn't allow live transparency at all.

Will flattening make my cover blurry?

Not if you flatten at high resolution (300 DPI or more). Check text and edges at high zoom afterwards.
