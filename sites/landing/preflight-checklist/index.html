Print it, tick it off

The book preflight checklist (24 checks)

Run through this list before you upload. Every item is something a printer really checks. If you can tick all 24, your files are in very good shape.

Quick answer

Check the interior (page size, margins, fonts, pictures, color, blank pages) and the cover (full size with spine, 0.125" bleed, safe zone, barcode space, spine text, color and ink) against your printer's own numbers, then save both as print PDFs.

Tip: decide your printer, trim size, paper and page count before you start. Half of these checks depend on them, and changing any one later means checking again.

Before you start

  • ☐ 1. Printer chosen. KDP, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press or Lulu. Their rules differ.
  • ☐ 2. Trim size chosen. For example 5.5" x 8.5" or 6" x 9". Your printer must offer it for your paper and binding.
  • ☐ 3. Paper chosen. White or cream, black and white or color. Cream is thicker, so it makes a wider spine.
  • ☐ 4. Final page count known. Count the PDF pages, not Word's page count. They often differ.

Interior (the inside pages)

  • ☐ 5. PDF page size = trim size. A 6" x 9" book needs a 6" x 9" PDF, not letter size. (If pictures run off the edge, add 0.125" to the width and 0.25" to the height.)
  • ☐ 6. Inside margin fits your page count. Thicker books need a bigger margin by the spine. See the table.
  • ☐ 7. Outside, top and bottom margins. At least 0.25" for KDP (0.375" with bleed), 0.5" for IngramSpark.
  • ☐ 8. Page numbers and headers inside the margins. They count as content.
  • ☐ 9. All fonts embedded. Check in Acrobat: File → Properties → Fonts. Every font says "Embedded".
  • ☐ 10. Pictures are 300 DPI at the size they print.
  • ☐ 11. Black and white book has no color. Blue links, colored logos and "black" text saved as RGB all count as color.
  • ☐ 12. No blank pages in a row beyond what your printer allows (KDP flags long runs of blank pages).
  • ☐ 13. Odd pages on the right. Page 1 and every new chapter you want on a right-hand page should be odd-numbered.
  • ☐ 14. Saved as a print PDF (PDF/X-1a is safest, especially for IngramSpark).

Cover

  • ☐ 15. Full cover size is right. Width = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed. Height = bleed + book height + bleed. How it's built.
  • ☐ 16. Spine width matches your page count and paper, from this printer's own numbers.
  • ☐ 17. 0.125" bleed on every outer edge. The background runs past the trim line.
  • ☐ 18. Words and faces inside the safe zone, well away from the trim and the spine folds.
  • ☐ 19. Barcode space left clear on the back: 2" x 1.2" for KDP, 1.75" x 1" for IngramSpark.
  • ☐ 20. Spine text allowed? KDP needs more than 79 pages; IngramSpark paperbacks need at least 48.
  • ☐ 21. Template guides deleted. Printer templates have colored guide lines that must not print.
  • ☐ 22. Cover art is 300 DPI at full cover size.
  • ☐ 23. CMYK, and under 240% total ink if you print with IngramSpark.
  • ☐ 24. ISBN on the cover matches the ISBN for this exact edition.

The checks that need special software

Items 9, 11, 22 and 23 are hard to see with your eyes. You need Adobe Acrobat Pro (Output Preview and Preflight) or a tool that reads the PDF for you. Questions people ask

What should I check before uploading to KDP?

Page size, margins for your page count, embedded fonts, 300 DPI pictures, full cover size with the right spine width, 0.125 inch bleed, barcode space and spine text rules.

Do I need Adobe Acrobat to preflight a book?

For a few checks (embedded fonts, ink coverage and true color), Acrobat Pro or a preflight tool such as SparkPrep is the reliable way. The rest you can check by eye with the right numbers.
