What SparkPrep checks (and what it fixes)

Here is every check SparkPrep runs, in plain words, using the numbers from your printer's own guide. Nothing here is borrowed from another printer.

Quick answer

SparkPrep checks trim size, bleed, spine width, safety margins, total ink (240% for IngramSpark), CMYK color, picture resolution, fonts, transparency, barcode space, spine text, leftover template guides and ISBN match. It explains each problem and can fix most of them, then export a print-ready PDF/X-1a file.

Interior checks (every page)

What you get

  • A plain-English list of every problem and where it is.
  • Fixes for the problems SparkPrep can repair itself. It only asks you for a new file when fixing would damage your art (for example, stretching a picture that's the wrong shape).
  • Print-ready files in PDF/X-1a, the format printers trust most.
  • A report. Books whose final files pass every check get The SparkPrep Certified Complete Publisher Preflight Report.

Which printers does SparkPrep check for?

Does the audit fix my files?

No. The audit finds and shows every problem. Fixing and exporting print-ready files is part of the book price, and the audit fee counts toward it.
