Real numbers

What it costs to get your first print book ready

Here are the real numbers, with where they come from, so you can choose with your eyes open. No option is wrong; each trades money for time and risk differently.

Quick answer

Hiring professionals typically costs about $1,730 for a cover and interior formatting (Reedsy's figures: median ~$930 cover, ~$800 average formatting). Publishing packages run about $2,000 to $7,000. Cheaper gigs exist on marketplaces, sometimes under $100, but quality and printer know-how vary a lot. Ask any freelancer: "Will you build this to IngramSpark's (or KDP's) exact rules for my page count and paper, and fix it free if it's rejected?"

The risk: a beautiful file can still be rejected. If it is, you wait for the freelancer again, and may pay again. Changing anything later (a typo, a new price on the back, a new page count) is another request.

Option 2: A publishing package

Companies like BookBaby sell all-in packages, roughly $2,000 to $7,000, with covers alone from about $399, and say to allow several weeks. You get hand-holding. You pay for it, and you'll still want to check who owns the files and the ISBN.

Option 3: Do it yourself with free tools

Word, Canva and KDP's templates cost little or nothing. The catch is that none of them check the printer's rules for you. Word can't make a true black and white PDF or a PDF/X-1a file; Canva can't measure the 240% ink limit. You learn by rejection. Expect several evenings of learning and at least one round trip.

Costs everyone has

  • ISBN: free from KDP (KDP-only). In the US, Bowker sells one for $125 or ten for $295 (check Bowker's current prices). Buy ten if you'll publish more than one format.
  • Proof copies: KDP charges printing cost plus shipping, usually a few dollars plus postage.
  • Editing: often the biggest cost of all, and worth it. Preflight doesn't check your words.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to self-publish a book?

Publishing on KDP is free. Getting it ready ranges from nearly free (doing it yourself) to about $1,730 for a professional cover and formatting (Reedsy's figures), or $2,000 to $7,000 for a package.

Do I need to buy an ISBN?

Not for KDP alone, which offers a free one. For IngramSpark or your own imprint, buy your own: in the US, from Bowker.
